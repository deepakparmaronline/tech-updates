Gemini does have photo upload limits, but the most important number is not a daily photo allowance. Google's current help page says you can add up to 10 supported files in one prompt, subject to availability. Those files can include images, documents, or videos, so the limit applies to the whole prompt rather than photos alone.

There is also a separate file-size limit and a separate storage limit for Gemini Apps activity. If you are trying to upload several pictures for analysis, understanding those limits can help you tell whether the issue is the number of files, file size, account usage, or saved activity.

## Short Answer

**Yes, Gemini has a photo upload limit.** Google says the Gemini web app can accept up to 10 supported files in one prompt, subject to availability. The official [Upload and analyze files in Gemini Apps guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178?hl=en) lists a maximum of 100 MB for supported non-video files. Photos count as files, so they use part of the same 10-file allowance.

That is a per-prompt limit, not a daily promise. Gemini also has account usage limits and a separate storage limit for previous chats and uploaded files. Google can change usage limits based on capacity and product availability, so the exact allowance you see may differ from another account.

## What the Question Really Means

People often use the phrase photo limit to describe different problems. One person may want to upload 10 pictures in a single message. Another may be trying to upload more photos across several chats. Someone else may be creating images with Gemini and reaching an image-generation limit.

Those are not the same allowance. The 10-file limit in Google's upload guide applies to a single prompt. It can include documents, images, and videos. The file-size limit applies to each supported file. The Gemini Apps storage limit applies to files saved in previous activity. General usage limits apply to how much you can use the service within a time period.

This guide focuses on uploading photos for analysis. If you are asking how many AI images Gemini can create, check the separate usage limits for image generation and the model or plan you use.

## How It Works

When you add a photo to a Gemini prompt, it counts as one supported file. Google currently lists up to 10 supported files in one prompt, subject to availability. A folder upload also counts each file inside the folder toward that limit. This means a prompt with six photos and four documents has used all 10 file slots.

The same official guide lists a maximum file size of 100 MB for supported non-video files. Videos have a separate size and duration allowance. The help page currently lists a 2 GB maximum for each video, with total video duration limits that can be higher on eligible paid plans. Those video rules should not be confused with the image file-size rule.

Gemini Apps also has a storage limit for saved activity, including chats and uploaded files. Google says this storage limit is independent from Google Drive or Google Workspace storage. If you reach it, you may see a message asking you to delete data before uploading another file.

There is one more layer: general Gemini usage limits. Google's [Gemini Apps limits page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en) says limits can change, including because of capacity constraints. It describes five-hour and weekly usage limits for some features and tells users to check the Usage Limits area in Gemini. Those usage limits are not the same as the 10-file prompt allowance.

## Practical Steps

### 1. Count all files in the prompt

Before uploading, count every attachment. If you add seven photos, one PDF, and two spreadsheets, you have reached 10 files. Do not count only the pictures. Remove files that are not needed for the question and upload the most relevant images first.

If the task needs more than 10 files, split the work into smaller groups. Ask Gemini to summarize each group, then provide the summaries and the most important images in a final prompt. This can be easier to review than sending a large collection in one message.

### 2. Check the size of each photo

Google's current guide lists 100 MB for supported non-video files. If a photo is larger, export a smaller copy or reduce the image dimensions before uploading. A normal camera photo is often much smaller, but high-resolution images and edited files can be large.

Keep the original file somewhere safe. Use a copy for resizing, especially if the image contains details that matter for inspection. Do not compress an image so much that text, labels, or small objects become impossible to read.

### 3. Check Gemini Apps storage

If Gemini says you need to delete data before uploading, the problem may be saved activity rather than the number of files in the current prompt. Google explains that uploaded files and chats use Gemini Apps storage, which is separate from Google Drive storage.

Open Gemini, go to Settings and help, then Activity. Review older chats or Gems that contain large uploads. Delete only items you no longer need. Google notes that it can take several minutes for deleted activity or Gems to free space, so wait and try again before assuming the limit has not changed.

### 4. Check your account usage limits

If the file count and size are within the published rules but Gemini still blocks the upload, check the Usage Limits area. On the web, open Gemini and use Settings, then Usage Limits. Google says Gemini notifies users when they are close to a limit and tells them when a limit will refresh after it is reached.

A paid plan can provide higher access for eligible features, but it does not remove every limit. Google's [Google AI subscription page](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/) describes different levels of usage access. Plan names, features, and availability can vary by country and change over time, so compare the current details in your own account.

### 5. Upload in smaller groups when a batch fails

If a prompt with several photos fails, try one or two images first. If those work, add more files until you find the point where the request stops. This helps separate a file problem from an account or capacity limit.

If one image fails repeatedly, export it to a common format and try again. You can also use the desktop web app instead of the mobile app to check whether the issue is tied to the device or upload method. Do not assume a single failed upload means the account has reached its daily allowance.

## Common Problems

A common problem is confusing the 10-file limit with a 10-photo daily limit. Google's help page describes up to 10 supported files in the same prompt. It does not say that every user receives exactly 10 photos each day. The daily or weekly usage limits are separate and can change.

Another issue is a file that is too large or not supported. A file may fail even when you have not used all 10 slots. Check the file type and size, then try a smaller copy. If Gemini says it cannot analyze a file correctly, Google recommends uploading it again.

A third issue is saved activity storage. Deleting a file from your computer does not remove a copy already stored in Gemini activity. If the storage warning appears, review Gemini activity and remove older chats or Gems that are no longer useful. This is separate from deleting files in Google Drive.

## What Does Not Work

Starting a new chat does not guarantee that the account storage limit will reset. The files from previous chats may still count toward Gemini Apps storage. Likewise, switching devices does not remove the account-level usage limits.

A paid plan should not be treated as unlimited photo storage or unlimited uploads. Google says higher limits may be available with a Google AI plan, but feature access and usage allowances remain subject to availability and change. Check the current help page before subscribing.

Do not upload the same large photo repeatedly if the error is caused by storage or capacity. That can waste time without solving the problem. First identify whether the issue is file count, file size, saved activity, or general usage.

## Safety and Limitations

Photos can contain private information, faces, location details, documents, or account numbers. Upload only files you have permission to share. Remove sensitive details when they are not needed for the task, and do not use a public AI chat as a permanent archive for private images.

The published limits are a guide, not a guarantee that every upload will work in every account. Google says availability and usage limits can change. Work or school accounts may also have administrator controls that affect file uploads and connected Google Drive access.

For the most accurate current details, check Google's [file upload guide](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/14903178?hl=en), the [Gemini Apps limits page](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805?hl=en), and the Usage Limits screen in your own account.

## Key Takeaways

- Gemini supports up to 10 supported files in one prompt, subject to availability.
- Supported non-video files, including photos, are listed with a 100 MB maximum.
- The 10-file allowance is per prompt, not a daily photo quota.
- Gemini Apps storage and general usage limits are separate from Google Drive storage.

## FAQ

### Does Gemini have a photo upload limit?

Yes. Google currently says you can upload up to 10 supported files in one prompt, subject to availability. Photos count toward that total along with documents and videos.

### How many photos can I upload to Gemini at once?

The published limit is up to 10 supported files in one prompt. If you upload eight photos and two documents, you have used all 10 file slots. The limit is not 10 photos plus other files.

### What is the maximum photo file size for Gemini?

Google lists a maximum of 100 MB for supported non-video files. Check the official upload guide for the current file types and any account-specific restrictions.

### Is the 10-file limit per day?

No. The 10-file allowance applies to one prompt. Overall usage limits and Gemini Apps storage are separate and can change based on capacity and product availability.

### Why does Gemini say I need to delete data before uploading?

Gemini Apps has its own storage limit for chats and uploaded files. Google says this is separate from Google Drive and Workspace storage. Review old Gemini activity and remove files or chats you no longer need.

### Can a paid Gemini plan upload more photos?

A Google AI plan can provide higher access to eligible features, including file uploads, but it does not make every allowance unlimited. Check the current plan comparison and Usage Limits screen before paying.
