Gemini can remember information from earlier conversations, but the answer depends on which Google account you use and which settings are available. Two controls matter most: Gemini Apps Activity, which manages saved activity, and Memory summary, which can hold details Gemini uses to personalize future answers.

These controls are related, but they are not the same. Turning off saved activity does not mean every conversation disappears instantly. A Temporary Chat is also not a password-protected or zero-retention session. This guide explains what Gemini can remember, what Google says it retains, and how to review the settings.

## Short Answer

Yes, Gemini can use past chats and a Memory summary when the relevant features are available and enabled for your account. Gemini Apps Activity controls whether eligible conversations are saved to your activity. Memory summary is a separate personalization feature that lets you review, edit, and delete what Gemini understands about you.

Google says Temporary Chats and chats made while Keep Activity is off are retained for 72 hours. That retention allows Gemini to respond to the conversation and helps Google protect its services, users, and the public. It is therefore more accurate to say that those chats are not saved to normal Gemini Apps Activity, rather than saying they are never stored.

If you want to stop Gemini from using details from an earlier conversation to personalize responses, Google says you can delete that chat. If Memory summary is available, review it separately and remove details you no longer want saved.

## What the Question Really Means

When someone asks whether Gemini remembers conversations, they may mean one of three things. They may want to know whether the old chat appears in the history list. They may want to know whether Gemini can refer to something they said in a different conversation. Or they may want to know whether Google keeps the conversation after they close it.

The history question is mainly about Gemini Apps Activity. The cross-chat personalization question is about Memory summary and the use of past chats. The retention question is about Google's data-handling rules. A setting can affect one of these areas without changing every other area.

There is also a difference between a personal Google account and an account managed by a school or employer. Workspace administrators can control some Gemini features and data settings. Availability can also vary by age, region, language, and product rollout. If you do not see a control described here, check the account type and the current Gemini help page before assuming the feature is missing.

## How It Works

Gemini Apps Activity is the account-level activity control. When Keep Activity is on, eligible Gemini activity can be saved to your Google account and used according to the settings described in Google's Privacy Hub. You can review and manage that activity through the Gemini Apps Activity page. The exact auto-delete period can depend on the setting you choose.

Memory summary is a different feature. Google says that, depending on eligibility and settings, you can view, edit, and delete what Gemini Apps understands about you. You can add details, correct inaccurate information, remove information, or turn Memory summary off. Google also says you can ask Gemini, "What do you know about me?" to view the summary when the feature is available.

Temporary Chats and chats created while Keep Activity is off have a specific retention rule. Google's Privacy Hub says these chats are retained with your account for 72 hours. Google uses them to respond to you and to help protect Google, its users, and the public. The page also explains that the last 24 hours of the chat can be used as context when responding. This is a short service-retention period, not the same as keeping the conversation in the normal activity list.

A saved conversation and a Memory summary can therefore behave differently. You might delete a chat from activity but still need to review a saved memory entry. Google says deleting a past chat can stop Gemini from personalizing your experience using information from that chat. The Memory summary controls provide another way to inspect what the system understands about you.

## Practical Steps

Start by opening Gemini with the Google account you want to review. Open the profile menu and find Gemini Apps Activity or the activity settings. Check whether Keep Activity is on. If you want future chats to stay out of the normal activity history, turn it off. Read the notice before confirming because the setting can affect features that rely on saved activity.

Next, review Memory summary if it is available. Open Gemini settings and look for the Memory summary section. Read the saved details instead of assuming the feature only stores facts you explicitly asked it to remember. Correct anything that is inaccurate, delete information you do not want used for personalization, or turn the feature off.

You can also ask Gemini, "What do you know about me?" Google lists this as a way to view the Memory summary when you are eligible. Treat the answer as a convenient view, not as a substitute for the settings page. Use the settings page to edit or remove information.

For a one-off conversation, start a Temporary Chat when that option is available. This is useful when you do not want the conversation to become part of your normal history or personalization. Remember the 72-hour retention rule. If the information is highly sensitive, the safest choice is not to enter it into an AI chat at all.

Finally, delete a past conversation if you do not want Gemini to personalize from it. Google says deleting that chat can stop that use. If a detail still appears in Memory summary, review and remove it there as well. For more context, read Google's [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961) and check your [Gemini Apps Activity](https://myactivity.google.com/product/gemini).

## Common Problems

A common mistake is assuming that turning off Keep Activity deletes old chats. The control applies to future activity in the way Google describes, but old activity may remain until you delete it or the selected auto-delete rule removes it. Review the activity list and delete the conversations you no longer want stored.

Another mistake is treating Temporary Chat as a session that leaves no data behind. Google says Temporary Chats and chats made with Keep Activity off are retained for 72 hours. This helps the service respond to the conversation and handle safety needs. The setting changes normal activity storage and personalization, not every form of short-term processing.

Some users also confuse Memory summary with the conversation list. Memory summary is a separate personalization view. A detail can be available in memory even when you are looking at a different chat. Review the summary directly instead of searching only the history list.

If you use a work or school account, an administrator may control some settings. A feature may not appear or may behave differently because the organization manages the account. Check the account selector and ask the Workspace administrator about the organization's Gemini settings if a control is unavailable.

## What Does Not Work

Turning off Keep Activity is not the same as deleting every old conversation. You still need to review and delete saved activity that you no longer want. Also, turning off activity does not automatically remove data from other Google services. Google's Privacy Hub explains that Gemini activity controls do not delete data stored elsewhere in your Google account.

Deleting a conversation is not a replacement for reviewing Memory summary. Google provides separate controls for the summary, including the ability to edit or delete details. If you want a detail removed from personalization, check both the chat and the summary rather than assuming one action changes everything.

A Temporary Chat is not a password lock. Someone who can use your signed-in device may still be able to interact with your Google account. Use the device's screen lock, sign out on shared devices, and avoid leaving a private account open in a public browser.

## Safety and Limitations

Do not use Gemini's memory as a secure record for passwords, payment details, private keys, or other information that should be stored in a dedicated secure tool. Memory is designed to make future answers more relevant, not to act as an encrypted vault. Keep sensitive information out of prompts unless it is necessary and you understand the account's data settings.

Review your account settings after major changes to the Gemini app. Google can roll out Memory summary and personalization features over time, and some options depend on account eligibility. The labels and location of settings can also change. The current [Gemini Apps Privacy Hub](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13594961) is the source to check when the app interface differs from an older guide.

The key point is to separate history, memory, and retention. Keep Activity controls saved activity. Memory summary controls a personalized profile when available. Temporary Chats change normal history and memory behavior, but Google says they can still be retained for 72 hours for service and safety. Knowing which control you need makes it easier to protect the right part of your conversation history.

## Key Takeaways

- Gemini can use past chats and Memory summary when eligible settings allow it.
- Keep Activity and Memory summary are separate controls.
- Temporary Chats and chats with Keep Activity off are retained for 72 hours.
- You can review, edit, delete, or turn off Memory summary when available.
- Delete a past chat and review the summary when you want to stop personalization from that information.

## FAQ

**Does Gemini remember what I said in another chat?**  
It can use past chats and Memory summary for personalization when those features are available and enabled. The exact behavior depends on your account and settings.

**Does Gemini remember conversations when Keep Activity is off?**  
Google says chats made while Keep Activity is off are retained for 72 hours for service and safety purposes, but they are not saved to normal Gemini Apps Activity in the usual way.

**How do I see what Gemini remembers about me?**  
If Memory summary is available, open its section in Gemini settings or ask Gemini, "What do you know about me?" You can review and edit the summary there.

**Can I stop Gemini from using an old chat?**  
Google says deleting that chat can stop Gemini from personalizing your experience with information from it. Review Memory summary separately if a detail remains there.

**Are Temporary Chats stored by Google?**  
Google says Temporary Chats are retained for 72 hours to respond to you and help protect the service, users, and the public.

**Does turning off Keep Activity delete my Google data?**  
No. Google explains that the setting does not delete data stored in other Google services. Review the relevant activity and privacy controls for each service.
