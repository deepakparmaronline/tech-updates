You can upload a photo to ChatGPT from a computer or phone and ask the assistant to describe it, compare details, read visible text or help with a task. On the web, use the plus button beside the message box, drag a supported image into the conversation, or paste a copied image. On a phone, tap the plus button and choose a photo or file. OpenAI lists a 20 MB limit per image, and the supported image formats include PNG, JPEG and non animated GIF.

## Short Answer

Open a ChatGPT conversation and look beside the message composer for the plus button. Select **Add photos and files**, choose the image and wait for the preview to appear. Type a clear question and send it with the image. On a desktop browser, you can also drag an image into the chat or paste an image copied to the clipboard.

OpenAI's [Image Inputs FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8400551-chatgpt-image-inputs-faq) explains image input formats and limits. Its [File Uploads FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8555545-file-uploads-faq) covers broader file upload rules. These are the best places to check if the interface or account limits change.

## What the Question Really Means

Uploading a photo is different from asking ChatGPT to generate a new image. When you upload a photo, you provide an existing picture for the assistant to inspect. You might ask it to describe a room, read a label, compare two product photos, explain a chart or identify visible objects. The assistant responds with text or another supported output. It does not change the original file on your device.

The upload control can also be different from a file attachment control in some product surfaces. An image may be selected from the photo library, a file picker or a desktop folder. The account may show different tools depending on the plan, app version, workspace settings and current rollout. If you do not see the same button as a guide, check the product you are using before assuming that your account is broken.

## How It Works

ChatGPT receives the image as part of the conversation. It can use visual information in the picture alongside the text you send. A short prompt such as "Describe the main objects in this photo" is enough for a simple task. A more specific request is better when you need a detailed answer. For example, ask it to read the visible ingredients on a package, compare the layout of two screenshots or list the steps shown in a diagram.

OpenAI's current image input guidance lists PNG, JPEG and non animated GIF formats, with a maximum of 20 MB for each image. A larger image may need to be resized before upload. The amount of image content that works in a conversation can also depend on the size of the pictures and the amount of text in the same message. Do not assume that every account has the same number of uploads at every moment.

A photo that looks clear on a phone can still be difficult to read after upload. Small text, glare, shadows, motion blur and low contrast can reduce the quality of the answer. If the task depends on a number, serial code, medicine label or contract detail, zoom in and verify the answer against the original. Treat the response as assistance, not as a replacement for checking the source image.

## Practical Steps

### Upload a photo on the web

1. Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) in a supported browser and start a new chat or open an existing conversation.
2. Select the plus button beside the message composer.
3. Choose Add photos and files, then select the image from your computer.
4. Wait for the image preview to appear in the message.
5. Write a direct question that explains what you want ChatGPT to inspect.
6. Send the message and review the response.

You can also drag a supported image file from a folder into the message area. If the image is already copied, paste it into the composer. If you attach several images, label them in the prompt, such as "Photo one is the front of the device and photo two is the back." This helps keep the answer organised.

### Upload a photo on Android

1. Open the ChatGPT app and select the conversation.
2. Tap the plus button beside the message box.
3. Choose the photo or file option shown by the app.
4. Allow access to the selected photo if Android asks for permission.
5. Pick the image and wait for its preview.
6. Add your question and send it.

The exact wording of the picker can vary by Android version and app release. If you do not want to grant broad photo library access, use the system photo picker when it is available and select only the image needed for the task.

### Upload a photo on iPhone or iPad

1. Open the ChatGPT app and enter a conversation.
2. Tap the plus button beside the composer.
3. Choose the photo library or file option.
4. Select the image and confirm the preview appears.
5. Add a short prompt and send the message.

If iOS asks for photo access, choose the option that matches your privacy preference. You can review app permissions later in Settings. You do not need to grant access to every photo just to ask a question about one picture.

## Common Problems

If the plus button is missing, update the app or reload the web page. Start a fresh chat and check whether image input is available in the account you are signed into. A work or school workspace may apply its own tool restrictions. If you have more than one account, confirm that you are using the account with the expected access.

If the file is rejected, check its format and size. Make a copy as a PNG or JPEG if the original format is unusual, then try a smaller image. Do not repeatedly upload the same file if the error message points to a quota or account limit. The [File Uploads FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8555545-file-uploads-faq) describes general upload limits, while the [ChatGPT status page](https://status.openai.com/) can help you check for a service issue.

If the preview appears but the answer is poor, the image may be too small or unclear. Crop the part that matters, use better lighting and ask one focused question. If the picture contains a chart, tell ChatGPT which labels or values matter. If it contains a long page of small text, upload a clearer scan or a document file instead of expecting the model to read every tiny character in a compressed photo.

## What Does Not Work

An upload is not a promise that every file type will be accepted. OpenAI's image input page lists specific formats, so do not assume that animated GIFs, video files or every camera format work as an image. If a file is not accepted, export a copy to a supported format. Keep the original unchanged in case you need it later.

Uploading a picture also does not guarantee exact text recognition. A blurry receipt or a distant road sign can be misread. For a decision that depends on a precise amount, address, serial number or date, compare the answer with the original image. If the image is too complex, split the task into smaller crops and ask about one section at a time.

## Safety and Limitations

Only upload a photo when the task needs it. A picture can contain faces, addresses, school names, medical details, payment cards, vehicle plates or private business information in the background. Crop out details that are not relevant. If you are using a company account, follow the organisation's rules for uploading client or employee information.

Review ChatGPT's Data Controls and the privacy terms that apply to your account. Do not assume that every consumer and business account handles content in the same way. If you are using ChatGPT at work, ask the account owner or administrator which information is approved for upload. Avoid using an unapproved personal account for confidential work simply because the upload button is available.

For better results, describe what the assistant should and should not infer. Ask it to list visible evidence separately from guesses. If the image contains a product, say whether you want a description, a comparison or troubleshooting steps. This makes the answer easier to verify and reduces the chance that a guess will be presented as a fact.

## Key Takeaways

- Use the plus button to add a photo, or drag and paste an image on the web.
- OpenAI lists PNG, JPEG and non animated GIF with a 20 MB per image limit.
- A clear crop and a focused prompt usually work better than a large group of unrelated pictures.
- Check account access, file limits and Data Controls before uploading sensitive content.

## FAQ

### How do I upload a photo to ChatGPT on my phone?

Open a conversation, tap the plus button beside the message box, choose Photos or Files, select the image and wait for the preview. Add your question and send the message.

### What image formats can ChatGPT accept?

OpenAI's image input help page lists PNG, JPEG and non animated GIF. If a file is rejected, make a copy in a supported format and try that copy.

### What is the maximum photo size?

OpenAI lists a 20 MB limit per image. The number of images that work in a conversation can depend on image size and the text included in the same message.

### Can I upload more than one photo?

Yes, supported accounts can attach multiple images. If the upload fails, try a smaller group and check the current file upload limits for your plan and account.

### Why is the upload button missing?

The control can depend on account access, workspace policy, product surface or app version. Update the app, open a fresh chat and check the tools available in your account.

### Are uploaded photos private?

Treat an uploaded image as information you are sharing with the service. Review Data Controls and your organisation's rules, and crop out sensitive details that are not needed.
