OVHcloud announced 55 new cloud and AI products on November 19, 2026, expanding its offer from GPU infrastructure to networking, inference, serverless services and enterprise AI agents. The French cloud provider presented the launch at its AI Summit. Its official newsroom page is stamped 12:00 on November 19, while Reuters reported the launch at 08:43 UTC the same day. The announcement is important for organizations that want more AI infrastructure hosted in Europe, but individual service availability and data-residency terms still need to be checked.

The products cover several parts of an AI deployment. OVHcloud named Nvidia RTX 6000 Pro, Blackwell B300 and Rubin R200 GPUs, new virtual private cloud services, AI Fabric, serverless tools, AI Endpoints, AI Agents and AI Search. The launch is broad, so buyers should compare each service rather than treat the 55-product count as one ready-made platform.

## News Summary

OVHcloud used its AI Summit to introduce 55 products across cloud and AI. The announcement covers infrastructure, orchestration, inference, networking and AI-augmented platform services. Reuters independently confirmed the launch and reported that the products run on infrastructure hosted in Europe.

The main change is the breadth of the offering. OVHcloud is positioning itself as a provider that can support more of the AI stack, from compute and networking to inference and agents. That may matter to organizations that want a European cloud option for workloads with data-location or governance requirements.

The company announced the products on November 19, 2026. Its [official newsroom release](https://corporate.ovhcloud.com/en-in/newsroom/news/ovh-ai-turn-55-products/) displays a 12:00 timestamp, without labeling a timezone. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/ovhcloud-launches-ai-cloud-products-hosted-europe-2026-11-19/) published its report at 08:43 UTC on November 19.

## What Happened

OVHcloud said the 55 products span cloud infrastructure and AI applications. The company highlighted new GPU options, an expanded virtual private cloud stack, AI Fabric, serverless services, AI Endpoints, AI Agents and AI Search. It also described security and deployment options for organizations that need more control over their infrastructure.

The GPU announcement names Nvidia RTX 6000 Pro, Blackwell B300 and Rubin R200 for Bare Metal and Public Cloud offerings. Reuters reported that OVHcloud said its data centres could take up to 60,000 B300 GPUs or 35,000 R200 GPUs. Those figures describe the capacity the company says it can support. They are not a promise that every customer can order that number of accelerators immediately.

The announcement also includes networking services such as a web application firewall, VPN, VPC peering, VPC endpoints, application load balancing, global load balancing and private DNS. OVHcloud says customers can manage public IP routing through BGP Service and bring their own IP ranges through BYOIP.

## Why It Matters

AI projects need more than a model endpoint. A production system also needs compute, networking, storage, identity controls, observability and a way to connect agents to business data. Organizations often assemble these pieces from several providers. A broader cloud catalogue can reduce integration work if the services fit the customer's technical and legal requirements.

European hosting is part of the story. Reuters reported that the new products run on infrastructure hosted in Europe. That can help organizations that need to evaluate where data is processed and which legal and operational controls apply. It does not automatically mean every service is compliant with every regulation. Customers still need to review the service region, contract, support model and any required certification.

The launch also reflects a shift from AI experiments to operational systems. Serverless containers, agent sandboxes, inference endpoints and private networking are useful only when they can be monitored, secured and operated at a predictable cost. The details of each service matter more than the headline count.

## Key Details

### Compute and GPU capacity

OVHcloud named RTX 6000 Pro, Blackwell B300 and Rubin R200 GPUs. The company says those options will expand its Bare Metal and Public Cloud offers. Buyers should check the exact hardware configuration, region, reservation model and service availability before planning a deployment.

### Networking and private infrastructure

The company described a wider VPC stack with WAF, VPN, peering, endpoints, load balancing and private DNS. It also highlighted BGP Service and BYOIP. These tools can help teams connect private workloads and manage network boundaries, but they still require a clear network design and security policy.

### AI Fabric and serverless services

OVHcloud says AI Fabric, built with Blade.sh, can launch agent sandboxes in Python. The public cloud catalogue also adds Serverless Containers, Serverless Functions and Messaging & Events. These services are aimed at teams that want to run workloads without managing every server directly.

### Inference and AI endpoints

The announcement says AI Endpoints is adding around one hundred models, including voice and image models, alongside governance tools. OVHcloud also described an integration with Gladia for transcription and voice generation across more than one hundred languages. Customers should check model names, regions, latency, rate limits and billing before moving production traffic.

### Agents and enterprise search

OVHcloud describes AI Agents as a platform for hosting, scaling, securing and monitoring agents connected to enterprise tools through nearly 480 connectors and MCP servers. AI Search combines semantic and lexical search across large document collections. These are company-described capabilities; the announcement does not provide an independent performance test.

## Who Is Affected

European enterprises and public-sector organizations are the most obvious audience. They may be evaluating where AI workloads run, which cloud providers can support GPU demand and how to connect agents to internal systems. Developers building inference services or agent workflows may also want to review the new endpoints and sandbox options.

Smaller teams should not assume that a large cloud catalogue is automatically cheaper or easier. A simple API call may not need a GPU reservation, a private cloud or a managed agent platform. Compare the full operating cost, including data transfer, storage, support, networking and security.

## What Happens Next

The next step is service-level verification. Check the individual product pages for launch status, regions, pricing, quotas, supported models and service-level terms. Ask which products are generally available, which are in preview and which require a sales agreement. For regulated workloads, review the exact service certification and contract rather than relying on a broad statement about European hosting.

Teams should also test the integration path. Run a small inference workload, measure latency and cost, confirm network access and verify how logs and data are retained. For agents, test connector permissions and sandbox boundaries before allowing access to business systems.

## What We Know and Do Not Know

We know that OVHcloud announced 55 products on November 19 and that Reuters confirmed the launch. The official release lists GPU, networking, inference, serverless, agent and search services. Reuters confirms the European-hosted infrastructure angle.

We do not know from the announcement alone whether every product is available in every country, what each service costs, how much capacity is currently unallocated or how each product performs under a customer's workload. The company has not provided an independent benchmark for the full catalogue. Treat the announcement as a product launch, then verify the specific service before making a purchasing decision.
