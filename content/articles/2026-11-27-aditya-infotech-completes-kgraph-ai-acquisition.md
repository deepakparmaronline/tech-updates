Aditya Infotech has completed a 30% stake purchase in KGraph AI Solutions, adding an AI video analytics business to its surveillance portfolio. The company disclosed the completion on November 27, 2026, for an aggregate cash consideration of ₹45.39 crore. It says the investment is intended to support commercial and technology collaboration with its CP PLUS surveillance products.

The update matters because it moves the deal from a proposed investment to a completed stake purchase. It does not mean Aditya Infotech has bought the whole company. KGraph has become an associate company after the 30% fully diluted investment.

## News Summary

Aditya Infotech's exchange filing says it completed the acquisition of 30% of KGraph AI Solutions Private Limited on November 27, 2026. The consideration was ₹45,38,85,993.60 in cash. The filing is dated November 27, and the NSE archive filename carries a timestamp of 18:23:33 IST. Multibagg's recap appeared at 18:26 IST.

The stated purpose is to connect KGraph's AI-based video analytics with the video surveillance products Aditya Infotech markets under the CP PLUS brand. KGraph's main product is BLUE, an enterprise video analytics platform. The company says KGraph's software covers compliance, automation, safety, monitoring and regulatory reporting for industrial and commercial sectors.

## What Happened

The filing is an update to an earlier announcement dated November 5, 2026. Aditya Infotech had already told the exchanges about the proposed transaction. The November 27 filing confirms that the purchase has now been completed.

The company acquired 5,798 securities out of KGraph's 19,325 securities on a fully diluted basis. That package includes 1,031 equity shares, 515 Series 1 compulsorily convertible preference shares and 4,252 Series 2 preference shares. The total stake is 30% of the paid-up share capital on a fully diluted basis.

The filing says KGraph has become an associate company of Aditya Infotech. That is different from saying Aditya Infotech owns or controls all of KGraph. The disclosed holding is a minority stake, and the company has not described the transaction as a full takeover.

## Why It Matters

The investment is designed around a product connection. KGraph's BLUE platform analyses video for enterprise use cases. Aditya Infotech sells surveillance products under the CP PLUS brand. The company says the planned collaboration will bring KGraph's AI video analytics into that product ecosystem.

Video analytics can help a business move beyond recording footage. A system can identify patterns, detect events and send alerts for a human to review. In industrial and commercial settings, those capabilities may support safety checks, compliance reporting, monitoring and operational workflows.

The quality of a video analytics system depends on the cameras, lighting, network, model, data quality and the rules used to interpret an event. A model that detects a person in a clear indoor scene may perform differently in rain, low light or a crowded site. That is why product integration and testing matter more than a headline about AI.

The exchange filing does not say that the integration is already available in every CP PLUS product. It describes a strategic investment intended to establish collaboration. There is no disclosed launch date, customer list, revenue target or performance result for the combined offering.

## Key Details

KGraph AI Solutions is an India-based private company incorporated on August 16, 2023. The filing identifies its industry as Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence. Its principal product is BLUE, an enterprise video analytics platform.

The company describes its software as covering compliance, automation, safety, monitoring and regulatory reporting. These are broad business areas, not a list of named customer deployments. The filing does not provide a detailed product feature sheet or a list of industries where BLUE is already installed.

The disclosed turnover figures are small compared with the purchase price, so it is important not to treat the deal value as proof of current revenue scale. KGraph reported audited turnover of ₹79.98 lakh for FY 2025-26, compared with ₹36.01 lakh in FY 2024-25 and nil in FY 2023-24. The filing does not provide profit, cash flow or order book figures.

The 30% stake is therefore a strategic investment in a business that Aditya Infotech believes can complement its surveillance products. The value of that collaboration will depend on future product work, customer adoption and execution.

## Who Is Affected

Aditya Infotech says the investment is intended to integrate KGraph's AI-based video analytics with CP PLUS surveillance products. If the integration moves forward, customers could see more software features connected to camera systems, such as event detection, alerts and monitoring workflows.

The company has not announced which products will receive the technology first. It has also not shared pricing, hardware requirements, service plans or the process for updating existing surveillance systems. Those details matter because video analytics can require compatible cameras, processing capacity, storage and network bandwidth.

A customer should not assume that every existing CP PLUS camera will automatically gain the same analytics features. Product compatibility is usually defined by the camera model, firmware, analytics engine and deployment setup. Buyers should check the official product specifications and ask for a demonstration using a scene that resembles their own environment.

The potential benefit is a closer connection between hardware and analytics software. The risk is that the value depends on reliable detection, manageable false alerts, clear privacy rules and long-term support. A camera that generates too many false alarms can create more work instead of saving time.

## What We Know and Do Not Know

Aditya Infotech says the purchase was made for cash and the amount was arrived at on the basis of a valuation report from a registered valuer. The company says the transaction is not a related-party transaction and that its promoters, promoter group and group companies do not have an interest in KGraph.

The filing also says no governmental or regulatory approvals were required. It confirms that the acquisition was completed on November 27, 2026. These details are part of the company's disclosure under Regulation 30 of the SEBI Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements Regulations.

The filing is useful because it gives the stake percentage, consideration, securities acquired and basic financial details of KGraph. It does not explain the valuation methodology or provide a forward-looking financial estimate for the collaboration. Readers should avoid turning the purchase amount into an implied revenue forecast.

## What Happens Next

The next meaningful updates would be a product integration announcement, a customer deployment, a pricing model or a disclosed commercial target. A demonstration that shows how BLUE works with CP PLUS hardware would help customers understand the actual use cases.

It will also be useful to see whether the combined products are sold as a software subscription, bundled with cameras or deployed as a managed service. Those models can affect installation costs, support and recurring revenue. The company has not disclosed those details yet.

For customers, the practical questions are accuracy, false alerts, data storage, access controls and how long video is retained. For investors and analysts, the questions include how the stake is accounted for, whether the partnership creates measurable sales and whether KGraph can scale beyond its current turnover.

The completion is a real corporate milestone. The commercial outcome still needs to be demonstrated.
