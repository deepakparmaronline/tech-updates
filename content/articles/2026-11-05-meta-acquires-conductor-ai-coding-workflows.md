Conductor's founder announced on November 4, 2026, that the AI coding agent startup is joining Meta. The founder's linked announcement post was timestamped at 23:48 IST on November 4, inside the current 24 hour window. The immediate change is for users: new sign ups have stopped, Conductor Cloud becomes read only on November 13 and the cloud service shuts down on November 20.

## News Summary

Meta has acquired Conductor, a developer tool built around teams of AI coding agents. The company announcement is important for two groups. Meta is adding a team and product focused on agent based software development. Existing Conductor users need to act because the cloud service is being wound down on a short timeline.

Conductor's official [announcement page](https://www.conductor.build/announcement) says the company is joining Meta and explains the transition. TechCrunch reported the acquisition and said Meta confirmed the deal. The reports do not disclose the financial terms.

## What Happened

Conductor announced that it is joining Meta. The product was built to help developers work with multiple coding agents and parallel tasks rather than treating an AI coding assistant as a single chat window. Developers could organise work in separate workspaces, compare agent output and manage coding tasks from one interface.

The official announcement is written by Conductor's founder and says the team has plans for a new interface early next year. It also says the product will keep working for now, but Conductor Cloud will shut down over the following weeks. TechCrunch's report on November 4 said Meta confirmed the acquisition.

The founder's announcement page is dated Wednesday, November 4. The linked founder post's X status ID resolves to 23:48:29 IST on November 4. TechCrunch published its report at 1:10 p.m. Pacific Time, which was 2:40 a.m. IST on November 5. Both timestamps fall inside the research window for this article.

## Why It Matters

AI coding tools are moving beyond one prompt followed by one answer. Developers are starting to coordinate multiple agents, give them separate tasks and review the work they produce. A tool that manages those tasks can become part of the development workflow, not just another model interface.

Meta's acquisition is relevant because it shows the company is investing in developer tools and agent based workflows. The announcement does not explain exactly how Conductor will be used inside Meta or what the future product will look like. It would be too early to claim that a new Meta coding assistant is ready or that Conductor's current product will become a specific Meta service.

For developers, the more urgent point is the service transition. Cloud workspaces have dates when they become read only and then shut down. Anyone who relies on Conductor Cloud should export work before the deadline instead of waiting for a future Meta product.

## Key Details

Conductor's official announcement sets out the immediate timeline. From November 4, new users can no longer sign up or download the app. Existing free users can continue using the product for free, and existing Pro users can keep their Pro features without paying while the transition continues.

On Friday, November 13, Conductor Cloud workspaces become read only. Users can still read old chats and export data, but they cannot send new messages. The company says active subscribers will receive a refund.

On Friday, November 20, Conductor Cloud shuts down and the company begins deleting Conductor Cloud user data. Conductor asks users to export their data before that date.

The local Conductor app has a different timeline. The announcement says it will continue to receive new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027. The founder says the local app will start winding down in early 2027 and that the team hopes to have something new for users by then.

The announcement does not publish a detailed future product roadmap. It does not say which Meta product will include Conductor's features, when that product will launch or whether every current Conductor feature will be carried over.

## Who Is Affected

Existing Conductor Cloud users are the group that needs to act first. They should identify the workspaces and chats they need, transfer important work to local where appropriate and export a full data archive before the cloud shutdown.

Developers using the local app can continue using it for now. They should still plan for the early 2027 transition and avoid making a long term workflow depend on a product that the company says will wind down.

New users cannot sign up or download the app from the date listed in the announcement. Teams evaluating coding agent tools should not plan a new deployment around Conductor Cloud. They should compare current alternatives and check whether those tools support the agents, repositories and security controls they need.

People who recently paid for Conductor Pro should read the official announcement. It says active subscribers will receive a refund. If a refund does not appear, users should contact Conductor through the support contact on its announcement page.

## What Happens Next

If you use Conductor Cloud, do not wait until November 20. The company says workspaces become read only on November 13. That leaves a short period when you can still view and export your data but cannot send new messages.

Conductor says you can move a cloud workspace and its chats to your Mac by right clicking the workspace and choosing Transfer to local. To download an archive of all your data, open Settings, choose Account and select Export data. Check the export after it finishes and store it somewhere your team can access.

If you use Conductor for work, tell your team about the deadline. Decide who owns each workspace, where exported files will be stored and which developer will check the migration. Keep a copy of prompts, project notes and code changes that are important to the project.

The local app will continue receiving new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027. Conductor says it hopes to release something new early next year, but it has not shared product details. Wait for a formal announcement before planning around that future product.

## What We Know and Do Not Know

We know that Conductor announced it is joining Meta and that Meta confirmed the acquisition to TechCrunch. We know the public transition dates for Conductor Cloud and the planned support period for the local app. We also know that the company says it will refund active subscribers and provides steps for transferring and exporting data.

We do not know the financial terms of the deal. We do not know the exact product Meta plans to build with the team, whether the Conductor brand will continue or how the current technology will be used. The announcement says a new interface is planned for early next year, but that is not a detailed launch commitment.

Conductor says it has never sold or trained on user data and says it will delete Conductor user data when the app shuts down in early 2027. Its timeline separately says Conductor Cloud data deletion begins after the November 20 shutdown. Users should export their data before the cloud deadline rather than rely on later access.

## FAQ

### Did Meta acquire Conductor?

Yes. Conductor announced that it is joining Meta, and Meta confirmed the acquisition to TechCrunch. The companies did not disclose financial terms in the reports reviewed for this article.

### What is Conductor?

Conductor is a developer tool for working with AI coding agents and organising coding tasks in separate workspaces. It was designed to help developers coordinate agent based work rather than manage everything in one chat.

### When does Conductor Cloud shut down?

Conductor says its Cloud workspaces become read only on November 13, 2026. The cloud service shuts down on November 20, 2026, and the company begins deleting cloud user data after the shutdown.

### Can current Conductor users keep using the local app?

Yes, for now. Conductor says the local app will continue receiving new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027. It expects the local app to start winding down in early 2027.

### How can Conductor Cloud users save their work?

Conductor says users can right click a cloud workspace and choose Transfer to local. They can also export an archive from Settings, Account and Export data. Do this before the cloud shutdown.

### Did Meta disclose the acquisition price?

No financial terms were disclosed in the official announcement or the independent reports reviewed for this article.
