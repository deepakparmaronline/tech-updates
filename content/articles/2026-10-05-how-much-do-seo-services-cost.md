A quote of $2,600 a month for 20 hours of SEO works out to $130 an hour. That sits inside the most common hourly band of $100 to $150 in Ahrefs's pricing survey, so the rate is not unusual. Whether it is a good deal depends on who does the work, what you get, and how results are measured.

## What Happened?

A user on r/SEO shared a quote of $2,600 per month for SEO services. The snippet we could see says the offer was 20 hours of SEO work per month. The thread was about 10 days old, with 104 votes and 334 comments. We saw only the title and snippet. We did not read the comments, so we do not report what people said about the quote.

The math is simple. $2,600 divided by 20 hours is $130 an hour. That is the number to compare with market data. We checked current pricing sources on 5 October 2026. Prices change, and surveys differ, so use the figures below as a guide, not as a rule.

## Why People Are Talking About It

SEO pricing has no standard rate card. A small local campaign and a large site migration are both called "SEO," but they are different jobs. Buyers cannot easily judge if a quote is fair.

Also, SEO results are slow and uncertain. That makes any monthly fee feel risky. People want to know if they are paying for time, for results, or for a promise.

Industry surveys help. [Ahrefs ran a pricing survey of SEO providers](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-pricing). It is a vendor survey, so it reflects what providers who answered say they charge. It is still the best public benchmark we found.

## What Users Experienced

We could not read the thread. Based on the quote and on public surveys, here is what buyers commonly face. Treat these as reports.

- Quotes vary a lot. Ahrefs's page reports that 78.2% of providers charge a monthly retainer, and that $501 to $1,000 per month is the most popular retainer range, chosen by 20.4% of respondents.
- Hourly rates cluster. The same page reports an average hourly rate of $111. The most common band is $100 to $150 (25% of respondents). The next is $75 to $100 (24%).
- Other sites repeat the Ahrefs data with different averages. One reports $3,209 a month for agencies, and another says agency hourly rates average $98.90. We could not match these to the Ahrefs page we opened, so we use only the figures on the Ahrefs page. Always check the original.

## Why It Happens

Pricing differs because the work differs. A fee can cover technical fixes, content, link outreach, reporting, and strategy calls. It can cover one site or many pages.

Who does the work matters. A senior consultant costs more per hour than a junior. A big agency includes account management and overhead in the rate. A freelancer may charge less for the same hours.

Location also matters. Rates in the US, UK and Australia are often higher than in other regions. Ahrefs breaks its data down by region, so check that table for your market.

Finally, SEO is hard to prove in the short term. Ahrefs's own study found that only 5.7% of new pages reached the top 10 within a year. So a provider who sells "results in 60 days" is making a bold claim.

## Working Fixes

Use this checklist to judge any SEO quote. It is our method.

1. **Convert the quote to an hourly rate.** Divide the monthly fee by the hours. For $2,600 over 20 hours, that is $130.
2. **Compare to market data.** $130 is within the most common $100 to $150 band in Ahrefs's survey. It is above the survey average of $111.
3. **Ask who does the hours.** Is it a senior expert or a junior? Ask for names and roles.
4. **Ask what is included.** Get a written list: audit, fixes, content pieces, links, reports, calls. Ask what counts toward the 20 hours.
5. **Ask what is not included.** Dev work, design, copywriting and tool costs are often extra.
6. **Check the first 90 days.** A good plan front-loads an audit and fixes. Ask for a clear 3-month roadmap.
7. **Agree on measures.** Choose leads, calls or sales, not only rankings. Use Search Console and analytics.
8. **Ask for proof.** Ask for case studies from similar sites, and check them.
9. **Check the contract.** Look at length, cancellation terms and who owns the content and accounts.
10. **Compare two or three quotes.** Ask the same questions to each.
11. **Consider a trial.** A short audit or a one-time project can show quality before a long retainer.

## What Doesn't Work

- **Judging by price alone.** A cheap retainer can deliver little. An expensive one can deliver little too.
- **Accepting ranking guarantees.** No source we found supports guaranteed rankings. Ahrefs's data suggests that most new pages need a long time.
- **Paying for hours with no list of tasks.** "20 hours of SEO" is vague. Ask for a task list.
- **Ignoring tool costs.** The provider may pass tool fees to you. Ahrefs and Semrush plans cost from roughly $129 and $139.95 a month respectively, according to pricing trackers that list these amounts. Check each vendor page, because plans change.
- **Skipping a contract review.** Auto-renewal and long lock-ins can cost more than the fee.
- **Mixing up agency, freelancer and consultant rates.** Ahrefs reports different averages for each group. Compare like with like.

## FAQ

### Is $2,600 a month for SEO too much?

It depends on scope and skill. It equals $130 an hour for 20 hours, which falls in Ahrefs's most common $100 to $150 band. Check what you get for it.

### What is the average SEO hourly rate?

Ahrefs's survey reports an average of $111 an hour. The most common band is $100 to $150. Local SEO is often lower, at $75 to $100.

### What is the most common SEO retainer?

Ahrefs reports that $501 to $1,000 a month is the most popular range, chosen by 20.4% of respondents. Many providers charge more for larger scopes.

### Should I pay for hours or for results?

Hours are easier to define. Results are hard to guarantee. Many buyers agree on hours, a task list and business measures such as leads.

### How do I know if the provider is good?

Check case studies, ask who does the work, and request a 90-day plan. Compare at least two other quotes.

### Do SEO providers guarantee rankings?

Be wary of any guarantee. Ahrefs's study found most new pages do not reach the top 10 within a year, so fast guarantees are a red flag.

### Are tools included in the fee?

Not always. Ask whether tool costs are included. Ahrefs and Semrush plans can add well over $100 a month, and prices change, so check vendor pages.
