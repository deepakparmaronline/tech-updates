If you are searching for how to get free Perplexity Pro, start with the difference between the free Standard plan and a promotional Pro trial. Perplexity does not list a permanent free Pro tier. Its current plan guide does allow trials and partner promotions, but those offers require a valid payment method and can renew at the normal subscription rate when the trial ends.

There are a few legitimate ways to reduce the cost: use the free Standard plan, check whether a trial is offered to your account, or apply for Education Pro if you are a verified student or educator. Some older promotions have ended. The Airtel offer in India, for example, stopped on January 16, 2026.

## Short Answer

You cannot activate a permanent free Perplexity Pro plan from the normal subscription page. The free Standard plan is available to everyone, while Pro is paid. A trial or partner promotion may provide temporary Pro access if Perplexity offers it to your account.

The official [Perplexity subscription guide](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11187416-which-perplexity-subscription-plan-is-right-for-you) explains the available plans and the rules for promotional subscriptions. It says a valid credit or debit card is required for trials and promotions. Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the plan renews at the standard monthly or annual rate selected during redemption.

## Understand the Free Standard Plan First

Perplexity's Standard plan is free. It includes search history, basic searches, a limited amount of Pro Search, basic file uploads and model selection by Perplexity. The plan does not include the full set of advanced models, image generation or premium support that Pro offers.

For light research, quick answers and occasional follow-up questions, Standard may be enough. You do not need to upgrade just because a tutorial says Pro is required. Try the free plan with your normal questions first. If you regularly hit its limits or need a feature that is only in Pro, then compare the paid plan with the work you expect to do.

The main difference is access and limits, not whether the free plan can answer questions. Standard can still be useful, but it has fewer advanced searches and file features. Check the current plan comparison before paying because feature limits can change.

## Check Whether Your Account Has a Trial

Perplexity's plan guide includes a section for trial and promotional subscriptions. It says a valid payment method is required to activate and maintain promotional access. The card is not charged during the trial period, but the subscription renews at the normal rate unless you cancel before the end date.

If a trial is available, it should appear in an official Perplexity sign-up, upgrade or promotion flow. Check the plan name, trial length, renewal date and price before you confirm. Save the confirmation email or take a screenshot of the offer details so you know when the trial ends.

Do not assume that every account receives the same trial. Promotions can be limited by region, account status, partner and time period. If you do not see an offer in your account or in an official partner app, do not rely on a code posted by an unknown website.

Set a reminder before the renewal date if you only want temporary access. Perplexity says the promotional subscription can auto-renew at the standard rate. If you do not want to pay, cancel through the official account or app store subscription settings before the trial ends.

## Education Pro Is a Discount, Not a Free Plan

Verified students and educators can apply for Education Pro. Perplexity says the plan includes Pro features plus education-specific guidance. Its current Help Center lists Education Pro at $10 per month, or a lower monthly equivalent when billed annually.

You can start the verification process from the upgrade area in your Perplexity account. Select the Education plan and choose Verify as student. Perplexity uses SheerID to confirm student or faculty status. The email used for verification does not have to be the same email used for your Perplexity account, but you must sign in to the correct account after approval.

The discount is available through checkout on perplexity.ai. Perplexity says it cannot be applied to subscriptions billed through PayPal, the Apple App Store or Google Play. If you already have an app store subscription, you may need to end it and subscribe again through the website with the same account.

Education Pro is not a free year of Pro. It is a reduced-price subscription for people who meet the verification rules. Read the current terms before checkout because eligibility and prices can change.

## The Airtel Free Pro Offer in India Has Ended

Perplexity and Airtel offered eligible Airtel customers in India 12 months of free Pro access. That promotion was popular because it gave users a full year of Pro features without a subscription charge during the offer period.

The offer is no longer active. Perplexity's official [Airtel promotion page](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11842322-perplexity-pro-airtel-promo) says it ended on January 16, 2026. Older videos and articles may still show the original redemption steps, but those instructions do not mean a new user can claim the offer today.

If you already redeemed the offer, the help page still provides recovery steps for an existing account. If you did not claim it before the end date, the Airtel card in an old screenshot or a shared redemption link will not reopen the promotion.

This is an important check before following a free Pro tutorial. Look at the date and the official promotion page first. A guide that was correct in 2025 can be wrong in November 2026.

## How to Check a Partner Offer Safely

Use the Perplexity website or app, the official Help Center and the partner's own promotion page. Check that the offer names the exact plan you want. A discount on Enterprise, a bundle of Computer credits or a statement credit is not the same as free individual Pro access.

Read the renewal terms before you add a card. Check the date the trial ends, the price after the trial, the billing period and the cancellation path. If the promotion requires a card, make sure you understand whether it will renew automatically.

Never share your Perplexity password with a seller. Avoid websites that promise lifetime Pro access, sell shared logins or ask you to install an unknown browser extension. A shared account can expose your searches, files and personal information. A fake promotion can also be a way to collect card details.

If an offer appears in a partner app, confirm that the partner is named on Perplexity's official promotions page. If the promotion has expired, do not enter payment details on a page that still claims it is active.

## Is the Free Plan Enough for Your Work?

The free plan is a good starting point if you mainly need occasional web searches, short answers and basic follow-up questions. It can help you decide whether Perplexity's source-led search style fits your work.

Pro is more useful if you need frequent Pro Search, advanced models, more file analysis, image or video generation and higher usage limits. If you use those features every day, compare the cost with the time saved. If you only need one feature for a short project, a trial may be enough if your account is offered one.

Students and educators should compare Education Pro with the normal plan. It offers the Pro feature set at a discount, but it is still a paid subscription. Businesses should check Enterprise pricing and privacy terms rather than using a personal Pro account for company data.

The best plan is the lowest plan that supports your real workflow. Do not pay for features you will not use, and do not chase a promotion that has already ended.

## FAQ

There is no permanent free Pro tier listed in Perplexity's current plan guide. You may receive a time-limited trial or partner promotion if Perplexity offers it to your account.

### Is Perplexity Standard the same as Pro?

No. Standard is the free plan with basic searches and limited Pro Search. Pro adds broader access to advanced models, file analysis and other features.

### Is Education Pro free?

No. Education Pro is a discounted paid plan for verified students and educators. Perplexity lists it at $10 per month or a lower monthly equivalent with annual billing.

### Is the Airtel Perplexity Pro offer still active in India?

No. Perplexity's Help Center says the 12-month Airtel promotion ended on January 16, 2026.

### Do Perplexity Pro trials require a payment card?

Yes. Perplexity says a valid credit or debit card is required for trial and promotional subscriptions. The subscription can renew at the standard rate unless you cancel before the trial ends.

### Can I trust free Perplexity Pro codes on social media?
