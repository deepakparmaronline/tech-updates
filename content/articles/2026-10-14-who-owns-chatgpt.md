## Short Answer

ChatGPT is made and operated by OpenAI. It is not a Microsoft product, even though Microsoft is one of OpenAI's major investors and technology partners. OpenAI's current structure has two main parts: the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation and the for profit company OpenAI Group PBC. OpenAI says the Foundation continues to control the Group.

The ownership details matter because people often confuse a company investment with ownership of every product that company builds. OpenAI's official [structure page](https://openai.com/our-structure/) says the Foundation holds a 26 percent stake in OpenAI Group, Microsoft holds roughly 27 percent, and current and former employees and investors hold the remaining 47 percent. Those figures describe the company structure at the close of the recapitalization described on that page. They do not mean Microsoft owns ChatGPT by itself.

## What the Question Really Means

The question who owns ChatGPT can mean several things. Some people want to know which company built the chatbot. Others want to know who controls OpenAI, who has invested money in it, or which company supplies the computing infrastructure behind its products. Those questions are connected, but they are not identical.

For the everyday user, the shortest answer is that ChatGPT is an OpenAI product. OpenAI develops the service, publishes its product information, and provides the terms and privacy details that govern its use. The [OpenAI About page](https://openai.com/about/) describes OpenAI as an AI research and deployment company and identifies the Foundation and Group as the two parts of its current structure.

A separate question is who owns shares in the company. That answer is more detailed because OpenAI Group has several equity holders. A third question is who provides cloud infrastructure or has a commercial partnership with OpenAI. Microsoft has an important role there, but a technology partnership does not automatically transfer ownership of every product to the partner.

It helps to use the right words. A product is a service or application. A shareholder owns a stake in a company. A board and its governance rules control important company decisions. A cloud provider supplies computing infrastructure. One organization can have different relationships with the same company without being the sole owner of its products.

## How It Works

OpenAI was founded in 2015 as a nonprofit. In 2019, it created a for profit subsidiary to help expand its research and deployment work. OpenAI says that the for profit company remained governed and controlled by the nonprofit.

On October 28, 2025, OpenAI announced an updated structure. The nonprofit became the OpenAI Foundation, while the for profit business became OpenAI Group PBC, a public benefit corporation. OpenAI explains that this type of company is expected to consider its stated mission as well as the interests of stakeholders. The Foundation kept control of the Group through special voting and governance rights.

The official structure page also describes the equity split at the time the recapitalization closed. The Foundation held 26 percent of OpenAI Group. Microsoft held roughly 27 percent. Current and former employees and investors held the remaining 47 percent. The Foundation also received a warrant that could provide additional shares if a future valuation milestone is reached. These details are more useful than a simple claim that one outside company owns everything.

Microsoft and OpenAI have worked together since 2019. Their official [joint statement from February 2026](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/02/27/microsoft-and-openai-joint-statement-on-continuing-partnership/) says their partnership continues across research, engineering, and product development. It also describes the cloud and intellectual property relationship. This is evidence of a deep partnership, not proof that ChatGPT is a Microsoft owned product.

The structure can change over time. That is why a current answer should use official company pages rather than old articles that describe an earlier investment arrangement. The OpenAI structure page is the best starting point for governance and ownership. Microsoft's own announcements help explain the partnership from Microsoft's side.

## Practical Steps

If you see a claim about ChatGPT ownership, check it in this order.

### 1. Confirm who operates the product

Start with OpenAI's official site and the ChatGPT product pages. Look at the company name in the terms, privacy policy, and product information. That confirms which organization provides the service to users. A product logo or an integration inside another app is not enough to establish ownership.

### 2. Read the current company structure page

Open the [OpenAI structure page](https://openai.com/our-structure/). It explains the relationship between the OpenAI Foundation and OpenAI Group PBC, including the Foundation's governance rights and the reported equity stakes. Pay attention to the date of the structure change because ownership stories can remain online long after the facts change.

### 3. Separate shares from control

A company can hold a large share of another company without controlling every decision. OpenAI says the Foundation has special voting and governance rights and appoints the Group's board. That control is a key part of the answer. A headline that mentions Microsoft's shareholding but ignores the Foundation's control leaves out important context.

### 4. Check the partnership separately

Use the [Microsoft and OpenAI statement](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/02/27/microsoft-and-openai-joint-statement-on-continuing-partnership/) to understand their business relationship. Read what the statement says about cloud services, intellectual property, and revenue sharing. Do not turn a statement about one part of the partnership into a claim about total product ownership.

### 5. Check the publication date

If an article was written before the 2025 structure update, it may describe an earlier arrangement. It can still help explain the history, but it should not be treated as the final word on today's structure. For a new investment or ownership claim, look for a more recent official announcement.

### 6. Keep product and API questions separate

ChatGPT is a consumer product. OpenAI also offers developer APIs and business products. A company may access OpenAI models through a cloud or API arrangement without owning ChatGPT. The route used to access a model does not decide who owns the model provider or the ChatGPT brand.

## Common Problems

One common mistake is saying Microsoft owns ChatGPT because it invested in OpenAI. The investment is real, but the conclusion is too broad. OpenAI's own structure page identifies Microsoft as a shareholder while also stating that the Foundation continues to control OpenAI Group.

Another mistake is treating OpenAI as a single nonprofit in every sense. OpenAI began as a nonprofit, but its current structure includes a nonprofit Foundation and a for profit public benefit corporation. Both are part of the structure described by OpenAI, and the Foundation controls the Group.

A third problem is using the word owner without saying what is owned. Microsoft has a stake in OpenAI Group. OpenAI develops ChatGPT. Microsoft also has a major technology partnership with OpenAI. Those statements can all be true at the same time.

A fourth mistake is assuming that a service available inside a Microsoft product must be owned by Microsoft. Companies license technology, build integrations, and work together in many ways. Product availability is not the same as company ownership.

Finally, do not rely on an unsourced social post that claims an acquisition has happened. A real acquisition would be a major corporate event and should be supported by official announcements or reliable reporting that points to those announcements.

## What Does Not Work

Searching only for a short answer snippet can hide the difference between ownership and investment. A search result may simplify a complex corporate structure into a sentence that is easy to repeat but incomplete. Open the source and read the relevant paragraph before sharing the claim.

Old news stories also do not work as the only source. They can describe a previous shareholding plan, a planned agreement, or a partnership that has since changed. Check whether the story was written before the latest official structure update.

It also does not work to treat a company partnership as an acquisition. A partnership may cover cloud infrastructure, research, licensing, or revenue. An acquisition is a different legal and business event. If a source does not say an acquisition occurred, do not rewrite it as one.

## Safety and Limitations

Ownership figures are time sensitive. The numbers in this article come from OpenAI's published structure page and describe the arrangement at the close of the recapitalization. If OpenAI publishes a later change, that newer official information should take priority.

This article explains company structure. It is not investment advice and it does not assess the value of any shareholding. A percentage stake also does not tell you every detail of voting rights, board control, or commercial agreements. For those details, read the official corporate documents.

The same care applies to privacy and product questions. Knowing who owns ChatGPT does not tell you how your data is used. For that, read the current ChatGPT privacy policy and the settings available in your account. Ownership, privacy, safety, and model performance are separate questions.

## FAQ

### Who owns ChatGPT?

OpenAI makes and operates ChatGPT. OpenAI's current structure includes the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation and OpenAI Group PBC, which is the for profit public benefit corporation.

### Does Microsoft own ChatGPT?

No. Microsoft holds a major stake in OpenAI Group and has a long running partnership with OpenAI. The OpenAI Foundation continues to control the Group.

### Who owns OpenAI?

OpenAI Group has several equity holders. OpenAI says the Foundation held 26 percent, Microsoft roughly 27 percent, and current and former employees and investors held the remaining 47 percent when the recapitalization closed.

### Is ChatGPT a separate company?

No. ChatGPT is a product and service provided by OpenAI. It is not a separate company that owns OpenAI.

### Why do people think Microsoft owns ChatGPT?

Microsoft has invested in OpenAI and has a deep technical and commercial partnership with it. Those facts are often shortened into an inaccurate claim that Microsoft owns ChatGPT outright.

### Is OpenAI still a nonprofit?

OpenAI has a nonprofit OpenAI Foundation and a for profit public benefit corporation called OpenAI Group PBC. The Foundation continues to control the Group under the published structure.

### Can the ownership change in the future?

Yes. Corporate structures and shareholdings can change. Check the latest official announcements before relying on an older ownership figure.
