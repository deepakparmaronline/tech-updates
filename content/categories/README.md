# Categories

The canonical category list lives in `lib/articles.ts`. Each article must use exactly one category.

`AI News` is the category for cross-industry AI developments, important models without a dedicated category, major new AI tools, and AI research breakthroughs. Its public archive is `/ai-news/`. Brand-specific stories should continue to use ChatGPT, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or Perplexity when one of those is the narrowest accurate category.
