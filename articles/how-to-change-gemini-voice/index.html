How to Change Gemini's Voice on Mobile and Chrome Step by Step

Change Gemini's spoken voice in the mobile app or Gemini in Chrome, understand language limits, and fix common voice selection problems for your device.

Short Answer

To change Gemini's voice on a phone, open the Gemini mobile app, open the menu, tap your profile or initials, go to Settings, and select Gemini's Voice. Tap a voice to preview it, then tap Confirm. Google's official Change Gemini's voice guide lists these steps and notes that available choices vary by language.

If you use Gemini in Chrome on a computer, the controls are different. Start Gemini Live from the Ask Gemini panel, then open More options and choose Select Voice. Google documents this flow in its Gemini in Chrome help page.

What the Question Really Means

People use the word voice to mean a few things. They may want Gemini to sound different when it reads an answer aloud. They may want a different voice for a live conversation. Or they may be trying to change the language or accent used by spoken responses.

The right setting depends on where you are using Gemini. The general Gemini Apps voice setting is documented for the mobile app. Gemini in Chrome has a separate selector for its Live feature. Do not assume that a setting from one product surface will appear in another.

Google also warns that the number of available voices varies by language. A missing option does not always mean the app is broken. The choices shown to your signed in account are the best guide to what is currently available to you.

How It Works

Gemini can respond with spoken output in more than one place. Google's mobile help page says the voice setting applies to Gemini Live and to features that speak a response, such as the Listen control beside an answer. This makes the setting broader than a single conversation.

Gemini in Chrome is a browser experience with its own Live controls. Google's instructions say the first time you turn on Gemini Live in Chrome, you choose a voice. You can return to the voice selector while Live is running and change it later.

Feature availability can change by region, language, device, and rollout. Google's Chrome AI update for India, New Zealand, and Canada explains that Gemini in Chrome and related features are being made available across supported devices and languages. Check the current help page for your exact product surface rather than relying on a screenshot from an older release.

Practical Steps

Change the voice in the Gemini mobile app

  1. Open the Gemini app on your phone and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Open the menu.
  3. Tap your profile picture or initials and open Settings.
  4. Select Gemini's Voice.
  5. Tap a voice name to hear a preview.
  6. Choose the voice you want and tap Confirm.

If you do not see Gemini's Voice, first check that you are using the Gemini app rather than a different Google app or a browser tab. Confirm that you are signed in and that the app is updated. Google notes that this setting is not available in every language.

Change the voice in Gemini in Chrome

  1. Open Chrome on your computer.
  2. Click Ask Gemini at the top of the browser.
  3. In the panel, select Go Live. Confirm any permission prompt shown by Chrome.
  4. While Gemini Live is active, open More options.
  5. Choose Select Voice and pick the available voice you prefer.

This is a separate flow from the mobile settings menu. If you cannot find Ask Gemini or Go Live, the feature may not be available to your browser, account, device, or region yet. Check Google's current Gemini in Chrome help page before changing browser settings at random.

Check the language

If a preferred voice is missing, review the language used by Gemini and your account. The set of voices can differ across languages. Switching language only to make a voice appear may change how Gemini understands your prompts, so choose a language you can comfortably use.

Test the setting

After confirming a new voice, ask a short question or use a spoken response feature. For Chrome, start a new Live interaction if the old session does not reflect the change. Test with a simple sentence before relying on spoken output for a long task.

Common Problems

The voice menu is missing. Check whether you are in the mobile app or Gemini in Chrome. The controls are not identical. Confirm you are signed in and that the feature is supported for your language and account.

The app says it cannot load voices. Start with basic checks: confirm that other internet services work, close and reopen Gemini, update the app, and try again. If the issue began after changing language or accounts, return to the settings you used previously and test once more.

The selected voice does not seem to change. Confirm the selection rather than only previewing it. End the current live conversation and start a new one. A feature may keep using the voice chosen when a session began.

A tutorial shows options you do not have. The tutorial may cover another language, device, account type, or version. Google's official help page should take priority over older screenshots and community advice.

The setting appears on a phone but not on desktop. That can be expected. The general Gemini Apps voice setting and the Gemini in Chrome Live voice selector are documented separately.

What Does Not Work

Clearing app data or reinstalling everything should not be the first step. Those actions can remove local preferences or sign you out without solving a server side or rollout issue. Begin with the product specific instructions, app updates, connection checks, and language settings.

Do not assume that a voice is available because another person has it. Google says voice options vary by language, and features can arrive at different times across accounts and devices. Do not use unofficial downloads or scripts that claim to unlock hidden voices. They can put account information and device security at risk.

Changing the voice also does not change the AI model's knowledge or make an answer more accurate. It changes the spoken presentation. Check important facts independently, regardless of which voice reads the answer.

Safety and Limitations

Voice input and spoken output can involve microphone, speaker, and account permissions. Review the permissions shown by your device and browser. If you use Gemini for work, follow your organization's rules for voice interactions and avoid speaking confidential information in a public place.

Voice options and availability can change. This guide describes the controls documented by Google at the time of writing, October 14, 2026. If the labels move, use the official help links above instead of guessing at a hidden setting.

Key Takeaways

  • Use Settings, then Gemini's Voice, in the mobile app.
  • Use Select Voice while Gemini Live is active in Chrome.
  • Check language and availability before treating a missing voice as a bug.
  • Restart the current conversation if a voice change does not apply immediately.
  • Prefer Google's current help pages over older screenshots.

FAQ

How do I change Gemini's voice on Android?

Open the Gemini app, go to the menu and profile settings, select Gemini's Voice, preview the choices, and tap Confirm. If the setting is missing, check language support and make sure you are signed in.

Can I change Gemini's voice on iPhone?

Use the Gemini app's settings if the voice control is available in your account and language. Follow the current Google help page for the labels shown on your version of the app.

Can I change Gemini's voice in Chrome?

Yes, for Gemini Live in Chrome where the feature is available. Open Ask Gemini, start Go Live, then choose More options and Select Voice.

Why are there fewer voice options in my language?

Google states that the number of voice options varies by language. Some choices may not be offered for every supported language.

Does Gemini remember my voice choice?

The setting is intended to control Gemini's spoken responses in the relevant product surface. If a session keeps using the old voice, confirm the selection and start a new conversation.

Is a paid Gemini plan required?

The general voice help page says users need to be signed in, but it does not state that a paid plan is required for the basic setting. Availability may differ for specific features, so check the current help page for the feature you use.
