If you search for Gemini Advanced, you may see older plan names and prices mixed with current offers. Google now presents its consumer AI subscriptions as Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra on the official plan pages. The price you see depends on your country, billing terms, and any offer attached to your account.

Short Answer

On Google's US plan page checked on October 26, 2026, Google AI Plus is listed at $4.99 per month, Google AI Pro at $19.99 per month, and Google AI Ultra starts at $99.99 per month. The same page also lists a higher Ultra option at $199.99 per month for a higher usage tier. These are the prices shown for the US listing, not a promise of the amount you will pay in another country.

The current official page does not show a separate subscription named Gemini Advanced. Instead, it lists the Google AI plans and the benefits included with each one. Check the current Gemini subscriptions page before subscribing. If you are in India or another market, use the matching local Google One page to see the plan and price offered to your account.

What the Question Really Means

People often use Gemini Advanced to mean the paid version of the Gemini assistant. That phrase can still appear in older tutorials, screenshots, and discussions, but subscription names and bundles change. The important question is which current Google AI plan gives you the features, limits, and storage you need.

The subscription is also more than access to a chatbot. Google's plan pages list Gemini app access and benefits across other Google products, with storage included at some levels. A plan may also include creative tools, research features, or higher usage limits. Those extras can make the plan useful for one person and unnecessary for another.

Do not compare only the name or one feature. Check the current plan, the country selector, whether a trial or promotion applies, the amount charged after the offer ends, and the included storage. Google can change model access and usage limits over time, so an old article may not describe the current offer.

How It Works

Google's current US page lists four main levels: Free, Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra. The free level is available with a Google Account. Google AI Plus is listed at $4.99 per month, Google AI Pro at $19.99 per month, and Google AI Ultra starts at $99.99 per month. The page also shows a $199.99 per month Ultra option with a higher usage level. Check the official plan page for the current terms.

The plans differ in more than model access. Google's page lists 400 GB of storage with AI Plus, 5 TB with AI Pro, and storage starting at 20 TB with Ultra. The higher plans also include more access to selected AI features and additional benefits across Google products. Exact feature access can depend on the country and account.

Practical Steps

  1. Open the official plan page. Start with Gemini subscriptions or Google One's AI plans page. Confirm the country selector and the name of the plan before comparing a price from another site.

  2. Choose the features you need. Write down whether you need higher Gemini usage, access to a particular model, research tools, creative features, integration with Google apps, or additional cloud storage. A paid plan is easier to judge when you know which benefits you will use.

  3. Check the local offer. The US page lists prices in US dollars, while other markets may show a different currency, price, trial, or bundle. In India, open the localized Google One page and follow the purchase flow to see the offer available to your account. Do not convert the US price and assume that is the amount Google will charge.

  4. Review the renewal terms. If the plan offers a trial or promotional price, check when it ends and what happens next. Confirm the billing frequency and cancellation method before you accept the offer.

  5. Check the account type. Google AI plans for personal Google Accounts may differ from AI options available through a work or school account. If your account is managed by an organization, check the product and billing rules for that workspace instead of assuming a personal subscription will unlock the same features.

  6. Compare the total value. Include storage and any bundled services you would otherwise pay for. If you only use Gemini occasionally, the free tier may be enough. If you regularly need higher limits or use the included services, a paid plan may make more sense.

Common Problems

One common problem is using an old price from a blog post. Subscription names and benefits change, and older pages may refer to Gemini Advanced or Google AI Premium instead of the current plan names. Use Google's current plan page as the final source for the price and included features.

Another issue is confusing a model or feature with a subscription. Seeing a feature mentioned in a product announcement does not prove that it is included at the same level for every account. Check the plan table and the availability notes for your country. Usage limits may also vary by feature and may change without the base subscription price changing.

People also compare a US price with a local price in another currency. That can produce the wrong answer because regional pricing, taxes, trials, and bundles may differ. Always check the local purchase page rather than converting a US price by exchange rate.

What Does Not Work

Do not assume that Gemini Advanced is a separate plan you can still buy under that exact name. The current official plan pages list Google AI Plus, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra. If an older article tells you to select Gemini Advanced, use the current Google One plan page to find the closest current offer instead.

Do not choose the most expensive plan just because it offers the highest limits. A higher tier can include storage and extra services, but those benefits matter only if you use them. Start with the tasks you need and compare the plan limits that apply to those tasks.

Do not treat a price shown in one country as a worldwide price. The official page and purchase flow for your region are the right place to confirm the amount. If the plan page changes after you read this guide, use the current official details rather than relying on the values in an older screenshot.

Safety and Limitations

Subscription prices, promotional offers, model access, and usage limits can change. The prices in this guide are the values shown on Google's US plan page when checked on October 26, 2026. They are included to explain the current plan structure, not to guarantee the amount that will appear in your checkout.

Before buying, review the renewal date, billing frequency, tax details, storage allowance, and cancellation options. If you use a managed work or school account, ask the account administrator which AI features are available. Do not share payment details or account credentials with third party sites that promise a cheaper subscription.

If you need help with an existing plan, use Google's official plan management help. It explains how to manage an AI plan from Gemini Apps. For practical Gemini use cases, see our guides to Gemini image generation and creating a project in Gemini.
