How to Cancel Perplexity Subscription on Any Device Safely

Cancel Perplexity Pro on the web, iPhone, or Android, check where you were billed, keep access until renewal, and avoid deleting your account first.

  • Uninstalling Perplexity or deleting the account does not replace cancellation.

The right way to cancel Perplexity depends on where you started the subscription. A plan purchased on the Perplexity website is managed through your Perplexity account, while a subscription billed by Apple or Google Play must be cancelled through that store. Check the billing channel first so you do not cancel the wrong place.

Short Answer

For a subscription purchased on the web, sign in to Perplexity, open your account settings, find the subscription section, and choose the option to manage or cancel the plan. If you subscribed through Apple's App Store, cancel it in your Apple account's Subscriptions settings. If you subscribed through Google Play, open the Play Store subscriptions page and cancel Perplexity there.

Perplexity's official billing and subscription FAQ explains the web, iOS, and Android paths. Its subscription plan help article also reminds users to cancel before deleting an account. Cancelling usually stops the next renewal; it does not necessarily remove paid access immediately. Check the confirmation and the date shown for your plan.

What the Question Really Means

A subscription can be billed by different companies depending on where you bought it. You might use the Perplexity app on your phone but pay through Apple or Google Play. In that case, the app itself may not have the control that cancels the store billed subscription. If you bought the plan on the Perplexity website, the cancellation option is managed in your Perplexity account.

This is why uninstalling the app is not the same as cancelling a subscription. Removing an app from your phone does not tell the billing provider to stop renewing the plan. Deleting your Perplexity account is also not a safe shortcut. Perplexity's own guidance says to cancel the subscription first so that you do not continue to be charged after account deletion.

Before you start, check the receipt or payment statement. Look for the billing provider, the subscription name, and the renewal date. That helps you choose the right cancellation path and gives you evidence if a charge appears after you cancel.

How It Works

Perplexity subscriptions renew according to the period and terms shown on the subscription page unless you cancel before the renewal date. The official Terms of Service explain that cancellation prevents future subscription charges, while paid access continues through the period already paid for. Refunds are handled separately and may depend on the purchase channel, timing, and local consumer rules.

The cancellation flow is different for web, Apple, and Google Play billing. The web route uses Perplexity account settings. Apple subscriptions are managed in the Apple Account or device subscription settings. Google Play subscriptions are managed through the Google Play subscriptions page. The exact labels can change as app and store interfaces are updated, so use the official help pages if a menu has moved.

You should keep the confirmation after cancelling. It can show the effective date and help you confirm that the renewal is turned off. If you cannot find the subscription in the expected place, do not start another subscription to solve the problem. Check the receipt and the account used for the original purchase.

Practical Steps

If you subscribed on the Perplexity website

  1. Sign in to the Perplexity account that owns the subscription.
  2. Open account settings and find the Subscription section. The official billing FAQ describes a Manage Subscription option under the subscription area.
  3. Choose Manage Subscription or Manage Plan, then choose Cancel plan or the cancellation option shown.
  4. Read the confirmation screen and complete the cancellation. Save the confirmation and check the plan's end date.
  5. If you do not see the cancellation option, use the support link in Perplexity settings or contact Perplexity support through the official Help Center.

Perplexity's official terms also describe cancelling through account settings or contacting support before the renewal date. If you cannot access your account, use the official support channel instead of sharing your password with a third party.

If you subscribed on an iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the iPhone or iPad Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.
  3. Select Perplexity from the list of active subscriptions.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription and follow the confirmation steps.
  5. Return to the subscription page to confirm the status or expiry date.

Apple's official subscription guide explains this process. If Perplexity is not listed, check whether you used a different Apple Account or paid through the Perplexity website instead.

If you subscribed through Google Play

  1. Open Google Play and sign in to the Google Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions. You can also use the official Google Play subscription page.
  3. Select Perplexity, choose Manage if shown, then choose Cancel subscription.
  4. Follow the confirmation steps and save the result.
  5. Check the subscription page again to confirm that it will not renew.

Google's help page notes that uninstalling an app does not cancel its subscription. If the subscription is not visible, check the Google Account used for the purchase and review your receipt.

Common Problems

If you cannot find a Cancel button, first confirm the billing provider. A subscription bought on the web may not appear in Apple or Google Play. A store billed subscription may not be cancellable from Perplexity's web settings. Use the original receipt to identify the right route.

If you cancelled but still see paid features, check the end date. Perplexity's terms say that paid access continues until the end of the period already paid for. This is different from an active renewal. A plan can be cancelled for the next billing period while the current paid period remains active.

If you are still charged after the expected renewal date, check that you cancelled the correct account and billing channel. There may be more than one subscription if you started a second plan through another provider. Review your receipts and subscription pages before asking support to investigate.

If you cannot access the original account, contact Perplexity support using the official Help Center. Do not give your password, verification code, or full payment details to a third party that claims it can cancel the plan for you.

What Does Not Work

Uninstalling Perplexity from your phone does not cancel the subscription. Both Perplexity's billing FAQ and Google Play's help documentation explain that app removal is not the same as stopping recurring billing.

Deleting your Perplexity account is not a replacement for cancellation. Perplexity warns users to cancel first. A deleted or disabled account can make it harder to find the subscription details, so complete the cancellation while you can still access the account.

Do not assume that a refund is automatic. Cancellation and refund requests are separate. The amount and eligibility may depend on your country, the billing provider, and how recently the charge occurred. Read the applicable official refund terms before submitting a request.

Safety and Limitations

Cancellation screens and menu labels can change. The steps here follow the official instructions checked for this guide, but use the current Perplexity, Apple, or Google Play help page if your screen looks different. Always make sure you are signed in to the account that paid for the plan.

Keep a copy of the cancellation confirmation and the next renewal date. If you believe you were charged after cancellation, gather the receipt, the billing provider, the date, and the confirmation before contacting support. Share only the information needed to identify the transaction, and never send passwords or verification codes.

If you need to compare paid features before deciding whether to cancel, read our guide to what Perplexity Pro includes or our overview of the Perplexity Android app. If you have already decided to stop the plan, follow the route that matches the original purchase.

Key Takeaways

  • Cancel through the same billing provider that started the subscription.
  • Web subscriptions are managed in Perplexity account settings.
  • Apple subscriptions are cancelled in Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Google Play subscriptions are cancelled through Google Play.
  • Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not replace cancellation.
  • Keep the confirmation and check the end date before assuming a renewal is stopped.

FAQ

How do I cancel Perplexity Pro on the web?

Sign in to the Perplexity account that owns the subscription, open account settings, find Subscription, and use Manage Subscription or Manage Plan to cancel. Follow the confirmation and save the result.

How do I cancel Perplexity on an iPhone?

Open Settings, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, select Perplexity, and tap Cancel Subscription. If it is not listed, check the Apple Account used for the purchase or look for a web billing receipt.

How do I cancel Perplexity on Android?

Open Google Play with the Google Account used for the purchase, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select Perplexity, and cancel the plan. Follow the current Google Play help steps if the labels differ.

Will I lose access as soon as I cancel?

Not always. Perplexity's terms say paid access continues through the subscription period that has already been paid for. Check the date shown in your account or store subscription page.

Does deleting my Perplexity account cancel the subscription?

Do not rely on account deletion to stop billing. Perplexity says to cancel the subscription before deleting the account so you do not continue to be charged.

What if Perplexity charges me after I cancel?

Check the confirmation, renewal date, billing provider, and account used for the purchase. If the charge still looks wrong, contact Perplexity support or the store that billed you and provide the relevant receipt and confirmation.
