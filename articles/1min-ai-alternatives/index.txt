<p>Before paying, verify the current plan and privacy terms directly with each provider. If you rely on a feature such as image generation, audio transcription, video creation, or model switching, test that feature specifically rather than assuming another product includes it.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-choose">How to Choose</h2><p>Start with a list of the features you use in 1min.AI over a normal week. Group them into writing and chat, document analysis, images, audio, video, translation, and social content. Mark each task as essential, useful, or rarely used. This helps prevent paying for a replacement that looks broad but misses the one feature your workflow depends on.</p>
<p>Next, test the alternatives on the same real tasks. Give each assistant the same prompt and source material. For visual work, use the same brief and output dimensions. Compare accuracy, editing time, export quality, and the amount of manual cleanup needed. If a tool uses credits, track the credits consumed by ordinary tasks rather than relying on a headline allowance.</p>
<p>Then compare the full cost. Include plan fees, credit top ups, storage, seats for teammates, and any specialist tools you would still need. A bundle may be cheaper if you use several features often. Separate subscriptions may be better if you need a small number of specialist tasks and want more control over each workflow.</p>
<p>Finally, review privacy and export rules. Confirm how uploaded documents are handled, whether you can export your work, and what the provider says about commercial use. Keep backups of important source files before migrating.</p>
<h2 id="what-to-avoid">What to Avoid</h2><p>Do not assume that one subscription gives you unlimited access to every model or feature. The product may use credits, limits, plan restrictions, or model specific rules. Read the current pricing and usage pages before paying.</p>
<p>Do not upload sensitive client files simply because a service offers document chat. Check the privacy terms and your own organization&#39;s rules first. Remove confidential details unless you have permission to share them.</p>
<p>Do not choose based only on the lowest monthly price. If a cheap plan lacks a feature you need, or each task consumes many credits, the total cost can be higher than expected. Test the real workload and review the terms before switching.</p>
