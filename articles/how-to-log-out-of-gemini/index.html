How to Log Out of Gemini on Web, Android and iPhone Devices

Sign out of Gemini in a browser, switch accounts on Android or iPhone, and learn when removing a Google account also signs you out of other apps.

Tech UpdatesNov 16, 20269 min read read
Key takeaways
  • Google documents a direct sign out option for the Gemini web app under the left menu and profile menu.
  • On Android, removing a Google Account from the device also removes its email, contacts and settings from that device.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Google documents sign out and account removal steps for Safari and Google apps.
  • Switch accounts when you only need another profile; remove an account only when you want to end that device session.

You can log out of Gemini from the web app by opening the left menu, selecting your profile picture or initial and choosing Sign out. On a phone, the right step depends on whether you use Gemini in a browser or the mobile app. Google Account sessions can be shared across Google apps, so removing an account from a device may also affect Gmail, Contacts and other services.

Short Answer

For the Gemini web app, Google documents a direct sign out path: open gemini.google.com, select the menu on the left, tap your profile picture or initial and choose Sign out. The steps are listed in Google's Gemini Apps sign in help page.

On Android, use the account switcher if you only want to use a different Google Account. If you want to end that account's session on the whole device, remove it from Android Settings. On iPhone or iPad, sign out from the browser profile menu or manage the Google Account inside the Google app. Do not remove an account unless you understand which other apps use it.

What the Question Really Means

There are two different tasks hidden in the phrase log out of Gemini. The first is signing out of the Gemini web app in a browser. The second is removing or switching the Google Account that the mobile app uses. These are not the same action. A browser sign out can end the Gemini web session without removing the account from the whole phone. Removing a Google Account from Android or iOS can affect several Google apps.

There is also a difference between signing out and deleting Gemini Apps activity. Signing out ends the session on a product surface. It does not automatically delete saved chats or activity from the account. Google provides separate controls for reviewing and deleting Gemini Apps activity. If your goal is privacy on a shared device, consider both the sign out step and the activity settings.

How It Works

Google says some Gemini web features can be used without signing in, while additional features and saved Gemini Apps activity require a Google Account. The account is therefore part of the service session. When you sign out of the web app, the page returns to a signed out state. When you use a mobile app, the account can be connected to the Google account session already present on the device.

This is why a mobile sign out can look different from a website sign out. Android manages Google Accounts at the operating system level. Google's Android account help page explains how to add or remove an account. It also warns that removing an account removes the associated email, contacts and settings from that device. The Google Account itself is not deleted.

On iPhone and iPad, Google apps have their own account management menus, and Safari can also keep a Google session. Google's iPhone and iPad account guide documents how to sign out of Safari and how to add, switch or remove accounts in Google apps. The exact menu can differ by app version.

Practical Steps

Sign out of Gemini on the web

  1. Open gemini.google.com in the browser.
  2. If the left navigation is closed, open the menu.
  3. Select your profile picture or initial.
  4. Choose Sign out.
  5. If the browser still shows a signed in Google profile on another page, check the account session separately.

If you use a shared computer, close the browser window after signing out. If the browser has several profiles, make sure you signed out of the profile that was used for Gemini. A separate Chrome or Edge profile can keep a different Google Account signed in, so do not assume that closing one tab ends every session.

Switch or remove an account on Android

If you only want to use another account, open the Gemini app and select the account menu. Choose the account you want to use, or add another account through the Google sign in flow. This is usually safer than removing the account from the phone when you still need Gmail, Calendar or Drive.

If you need to remove the account from the device, open Android Settings and look for Passwords and accounts. Some phones use Users and accounts, Manage accounts or a similar label. Select the Google Account, choose Remove account and confirm. Google's help page notes that the account's email, contacts and settings are removed from the device. This does not delete the Google Account online, but it can sign the device out of other Google services that use the same account.

Sign out on iPhone or iPad

If you use Gemini through Safari, open google.com, tap your profile picture and select Sign out. Google's iPhone guide says that signing in to a Google app or third party product can also sign you in to Google in Safari. You may need to sign out from the browser profile menu as well as the app.

If you use a Google app, open the app, tap the profile picture and select the account that is signed in. The Google Account guide explains that you can manage or remove the account from the app. If your goal is to switch, select another account instead of removing the one you use for other services. If you remove the only Google Account from the Google apps on the device, Google says the device will be signed out.

Common Problems

If the Sign out option is missing, confirm that you are on gemini.google.com and that the left menu is open. A browser window may show a different account profile if several Google accounts are active. Select the correct profile before signing out. If you use a work or school account, an administrator may control access to Gemini and some account settings.

If you sign out but Gemini still opens with an account, check whether the browser is using a different profile or whether another Google app is signing you in automatically. On iPhone, Google documents automatic sign in to Safari when you sign in to a Google app or third party product. On Android, the operating system can keep a Google Account on the device even after you close the Gemini app.

If you cannot use Gemini after signing out, that may be expected. Google says some web features are available without signing in, but additional features and saved activity require a Google Account. Sign in again only when you want to use those features. If the account is managed by a school or employer, check the organisation's policy.

What Does Not Work

Uninstalling the Gemini app is not a reliable way to sign out of every Google session. The account may still be signed in to Chrome, Gmail or the Google app. Clearing browser data can sign you out of many websites, but it is broader than a Gemini only sign out and may remove useful saved sessions.

Deleting Gemini Apps activity is also not the same as signing out. Activity controls manage saved data, while the sign out control ends the current session. If you want to remove old chats, use Google's activity management page separately. If you only want to change accounts, use the account switcher rather than deleting data.

Safety and Limitations

Removing a Google Account from a phone can affect email, contacts, calendars and other synced data on that device. Before you do it, make sure you know the account password and have another way to access important information. If the phone belongs to your employer or school, follow its account management rules rather than removing a managed account without permission.

For a shared computer, signing out of Gemini is a good first step, but it is not a substitute for using a separate browser profile or guest session. Avoid saving passwords on a public computer. If you are concerned about old chats, review Gemini Apps activity and its retention settings. Signing out alone does not promise that account activity has been deleted.

Key Takeaways

  • The Gemini web app has a direct sign out option in the profile menu.
  • Mobile sign out depends on the Google Account session used by the app or browser.
  • Removing an account from Android can remove email, contacts and settings from the device.
  • Use account switching when you only need another profile, and review activity settings separately.

FAQ

How do I log out of Gemini on a computer?

Open gemini.google.com, select the left menu, tap your profile picture or initial and choose Sign out. Google lists these steps in its Gemini Apps sign in help page.

Can I sign out of Gemini without signing out of Gmail?

The Gemini web app has its own sign out control. Removing a Google Account from a phone is broader and can also remove email, contacts and settings from that device.

How do I log out of Gemini on Android?

Switch accounts in the app if you only need another profile. To end the Google Account session on the device, remove the account from Android Settings, knowing that other Google apps will be affected.

How do I log out of Gemini on iPhone?

If you use Gemini in Safari, sign out from the Google profile menu. If you use a Google app account session, manage or remove the account from the app's profile menu.

Does uninstalling Gemini sign me out?

Uninstalling an app does not necessarily end the Google Account session in the browser or other Google apps. Use the account sign out or removal controls instead.

Does signing out delete Gemini chats?

No. Signing out ends the session. Use Gemini Apps activity controls separately if you want to review or delete saved activity.
