How to Make ChatGPT Have a Personality: Settings and Prompts

Set a ChatGPT personality with Personalization, response characteristics and custom instructions. Learn what each setting changes and what it cannot change.

Tech UpdatesNov 19, 20268 min read
Key takeaways
  • The Base style and tone setting changes how ChatGPT communicates, not its core capabilities or safety rules.
  • Characteristics can fine-tune warmth, enthusiasm, formatting and emoji use alongside a selected personality.
  • Custom instructions are the place to describe your own response preferences, context and boundaries.
  • Short, testable instructions usually work better than a long list of conflicting style demands.

You can make ChatGPT sound more like the assistant you want by changing its personality and adding clear custom instructions. The main setting is in Personalization, where you can choose a Base style and tone. You can then fine-tune characteristics such as warmth, enthusiasm, formatting and emoji use. Custom instructions let you explain your preferred language, level of detail and work context.

These settings do not create a new model or remove safety rules. They guide the way ChatGPT communicates. The best setup is short, clear and easy to test. You do not need a complicated prompt to make the assistant more direct, friendly, formal or concise.

Short Answer

Open ChatGPT, go to Personalization and choose a Base style and tone. Select the style that is closest to the way you want answers to sound. If the available characteristics are shown in your account, adjust them next. Then add custom instructions for details that a preset cannot express, such as your preferred language, formatting, work context or how you want technical topics explained.

OpenAI describes personality as a response style. It does not change the assistant's abilities or the safety rules it follows. A selected personality also does not control every piece of content. If you ask for a formal email, a code snippet or a structured report, the task itself can shape the result.

What the Question Really Means

People use the phrase ChatGPT personality in different ways. Some want a warmer assistant that feels less robotic. Others want short answers, professional language, fewer emojis or a consistent way of explaining technical subjects. Some users want a character voice for creative writing. Those are related but different goals.

A preset personality is a starting point. Characteristics provide smaller adjustments. Custom instructions explain the user's own preferences and context. A conversation prompt can still change the style for one task. Understanding these layers helps you avoid repeating the same instruction in every chat.

It is also important to separate personality from memory and model capability. A friendly style does not give the assistant more knowledge. A concise style does not guarantee that an answer is correct. If you want ChatGPT to remember a preference across chats, check the memory and personalization controls available to your account. If you want a different model or tool, choose that separately.

How It Works

OpenAI's personality guide explains that the selected personality influences tone and behavior. It can make responses feel more friendly, professional, direct or playful. It does not change what the model can do or the safety rules it follows.

The separate Characteristics guide describes controls for warmth, enthusiasm, formatting and emoji use. These characteristics work alongside the selected personality and custom instructions. They are adjustments, not a separate model.

Custom Instructions let you provide information that ChatGPT should consider in its responses. You can describe your role, preferred language, typical audience, level of detail and formatting needs. OpenAI says the instructions apply across chats and are available on web, desktop, iOS and Android.

The three layers can work together. For example, you might choose Professional, reduce enthusiasm, and add an instruction to explain technical concepts in plain English with a short example. If a task needs a different style, say so in that conversation.

Practical Steps

1. Open Personalization

On the web, open your profile menu and select Personalization. Find the Base style and tone control. On iOS or Android, open your profile menu and go to Personalization. The exact position of controls can change as the app is updated, so use the current Help Center instructions if a label looks different.

2. Choose a starting personality

Pick the option that is closest to your everyday preference. Professional is useful for work messages and documentation. Friendly can suit conversational help. Candid is useful when you want direct feedback. Default is a neutral starting point. Other styles may appear in your account.

Do not spend too long trying every preset. Choose one, test it with a few real prompts and adjust only if you notice a consistent problem.

3. Adjust characteristics

If your account shows Characteristics, use them to fine-tune the response. You may be able to make answers warmer or less enthusiastic, change formatting or reduce emoji use. These controls are useful when a preset is almost right but still feels too chatty, too formal or too decorated.

4. Write useful custom instructions

Open the Custom Instructions area and write a few clear preferences. A practical structure is:

  • Your context: what you do and the subjects you often ask about.
  • Your style: simple language, direct answers or a professional tone.
  • Your format: short paragraphs, steps, tables or examples when useful.
  • Your limits: ask before assuming a missing detail and say when information is uncertain.

Avoid writing a long personality story. A few specific rules are easier to follow and easier to update.

5. Test with the same prompts

Use three prompts that represent your real work. Ask for an explanation, a short email and a technical answer. Compare the results before and after the change. Look at sentence length, detail, tone and formatting rather than judging only whether the answer sounds friendly.

6. Add task-specific instructions when needed

A global personality should not control every task. If you want a formal client email, say so in that request. If you want a short answer, ask for a short answer. A task-specific instruction can guide the current response without requiring you to change your general settings.

7. Review your settings occasionally

Your work may change. A style that helps with brainstorming may not be suitable for reports. Review the instructions when the answers start feeling repetitive or when you use ChatGPT for a different kind of task. Remove rules that no longer help.

Common Problems

A common problem is conflicting instructions. You may select a concise style and then add a custom instruction asking for detailed explanations. The assistant may not know which preference matters most for a particular task. Make the priority clear, such as: keep normal answers short, but give detailed steps for technical tutorials.

Another issue is expecting a personality preset to change every output. OpenAI notes that the requested format and context can take priority. A code snippet, spreadsheet formula or formal letter may not show the same tone as a casual conversation.

You may also see different controls on web and mobile. Update the app, check the Personalization page and follow the platform-specific Help Center instructions. Do not assume a feature is missing simply because it is in a different menu.

What Does Not Work

A personality setting cannot make ChatGPT always agree with you, guarantee a particular answer or remove its safety rules. It does not turn a general model into a specialist with verified professional credentials. It also cannot guarantee that every response will use the same vocabulary or sentence structure.

A very long instruction list is not a reliable substitute for a clear task. Avoid contradictory demands such as always be brief and always explain every detail. Give the assistant a default style and explain when it should make an exception.

Do not treat a friendly or confident voice as proof that an answer is accurate. Verify important claims with primary sources, especially when a response involves money, law, health, security or business decisions.

Safety and Limitations

Custom instructions can include personal context, so share only what is useful. OpenAI's help page explains how custom instructions are applied and how they relate to data use. Review the current data controls before adding sensitive details. Do not place passwords, authentication codes, customer records or confidential company information in a personality instruction.

A personality setting is also not a security control. It cannot prevent a user from sharing sensitive information or guarantee that the model will never make a mistake. Keep access to files and connected tools limited to what a task needs. For important work, check the output before you send or publish it.

Key Takeaways

  • Start with Base style and tone in Personalization.
  • Use Characteristics for smaller adjustments to warmth, formatting and emoji use.
  • Use Custom Instructions for your own context and response rules.
  • Keep instructions short and test them on real tasks.
  • A personality changes communication style, not capabilities or safety rules.

FAQ

Can I give ChatGPT a custom personality?

Yes. Choose a Base style and tone, adjust available characteristics and add custom instructions for preferences that are not covered by a preset.

Does ChatGPT personality change the model?

No. It guides communication style. It does not change the model's core capabilities or safety rules.

Where are ChatGPT personality settings?

On the web, open your profile menu and select Personalization. On mobile, open your profile menu and then Personalization.

Are custom instructions available on all plans?

OpenAI says custom instructions are available across plans on web, desktop, iOS and Android. Check the current Help Center for limits that apply to your account.

Why does ChatGPT sometimes ignore my personality?

A task-specific request or other context can influence the response. The personality is a guide, not a guarantee for every format.

Should I add private details to custom instructions?

Only add information you are comfortable sharing with the service. Avoid passwords, customer data and confidential business information.
