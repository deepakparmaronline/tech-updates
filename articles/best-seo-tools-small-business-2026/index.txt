19:T2656,<p>Most small businesses can start SEO with free tools: Google Search Console, Google Trends, Keyword Planner and the free version of Screaming Frog. Paid suites like Ahrefs and Semrush cost roughly $129 to $140 a month at entry level, and are worth it only when free data stops answering your questions.</p>
<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened?</h2><p>A user on r/seotoolsforsmbs posted a guide called &quot;Best SEO tools for small businesses in 2026, actual prices, no BS.&quot; The thread was about two months old, with 5 votes and 27 comments. The snippet we could read said that for most small businesses just starting SEO, you do not need Ahrefs or Semrush yet. We did not open the thread or read the comments, so we do not report its price list or replies.</p>
<p>Instead, we checked current prices ourselves on 5 October 2026. All prices below come from vendor pages or trackers we found in search. Tool prices change often, so confirm them on each vendor&#39;s page before you buy.</p>
<h2 id="why-people-are-talking-about-it">Why People Are Talking About It</h2><p>Small business owners do not have big budgets. They see long lists of SEO tools with high prices and do not know where to begin.</p>
<p>Tool vendors also change plans often. In 2026, both Ahrefs and Semrush have more than one pricing ladder. That makes it harder to compare. Search results are full of affiliate pages that may favor tools that pay commissions.</p>
<p>An honest answer helps: start with free tools, learn what question you cannot answer, then pay for a tool that solves that question.</p>
<h2 id="what-users-experienced">What Users Experienced</h2><p>We could not read the thread. Here is what we found in public price sources. Treat third-party figures as reports, since trackers can lag behind vendors.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Ahrefs.</strong> TechRadar&#39;s review says Ahrefs offers a Starter plan at $29 a month, Lite at $129 ($108 if billed yearly), Standard at $249 ($208 yearly), and Enterprise at $1,499. It also says Ahrefs offers a free Webmaster Tools plan with limited access for site owners. Other trackers agree on the $29, $129 and $249 steps. One source says seats and add-ons cost extra.</li>
<li><strong>Semrush.</strong> A DEV Community guide that lists October 2026 prices says Semrush plans run $139.95 for Pro, $249.95 for Guru and $499.95 for Business, with a newer lineup next to it. Other trackers give slightly different figures, such as $129.95 for Pro, so check the vendor page.</li>
<li><strong>Screaming Frog.</strong> The vendor&#39;s page says the SEO Spider is free for up to 500 URLs, and a licence costs £199 per year. Licences last one year.</li>
<li><strong>Google tools.</strong> Google&#39;s help page says Search Console is a free service. TechRadar&#39;s review of Google&#39;s tools lists Google Trends as free and Keyword Planner as free but needing a Google Ads account.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="why-it-happens">Why It Happens</h2><p>Paid SEO suites are built for agencies and in-house teams that track many sites and keywords. They give competitor data, backlink data and difficulty scores. A small business with one site often needs only a fraction of that.</p>
<p>Free tools cover the first questions well. <a href="https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/35120">Google&#39;s Search Console help</a> says it helps you confirm that Google can find and crawl your site, see search traffic data and queries, and receive alerts about indexing or spam issues. That covers much of the day-to-day need for a small site.</p>
<p>Where free tools fall short is in competitor and backlink research. Search Console shows only your own site. Keyword Planner shows volume as ranges and was built for ads, as Ahrefs notes in its own guide. If you need to see who ranks and why, a paid tool helps.</p>
<h2 id="working-fixes">Working Fixes</h2><p>Use this simple ladder. It is our recommendation, based on the prices and tool limits above.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Start at $0.</strong> Set up Google Search Console and verify your site. Use Google Trends to compare terms. Use Keyword Planner for rough ideas. Use free analytics to see what visitors do.</li>
<li><strong>Add a free crawler.</strong> Use the free version of Screaming Frog to crawl up to 500 URLs. This fits most small sites and finds broken links, missing titles and duplicate pages.</li>
<li><strong>Try the free tier of a suite.</strong> Ahrefs offers a free plan for verified sites, per TechRadar. It adds backlink and audit data for your own site.</li>
<li><strong>Define the question first.</strong> Write down what you cannot answer now. Examples: who links to my competitors, which keywords do they rank for, how does my rank change weekly.</li>
<li><strong>Upgrade only for that question.</strong> If you need competitor and keyword data, compare an entry plan from Ahrefs or Semrush. Also compare the cheaper Ahrefs Starter at $29 if it covers your use.</li>
<li><strong>Check hidden costs.</strong> Seats, add-ons and credit limits can raise the real bill. Ahrefs&#39;s higher plans have extra seat fees on some trackers.</li>
<li><strong>Pay monthly first.</strong> Annual billing saves money, but only if you will use the tool for a year.</li>
<li><strong>Review in three months.</strong> If you did not log in often, cancel.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="what-doesn-t-work">What Doesn't Work</h2><ul>
<li><strong>Buying a suite on day one.</strong> You pay for features you do not use.</li>
<li><strong>Trusting a tool score as truth.</strong> Volume and difficulty are estimates, and tools differ.</li>
<li><strong>Using affiliate lists as neutral advice.</strong> Many &quot;best tools&quot; pages earn commissions.</li>
<li><strong>Paying for AI add-ons you do not need.</strong> Some trackers list extra monthly fees for AI content or tracking add-ons.</li>
<li><strong>Ignoring seats and limits.</strong> A cheap plan may cap projects, keywords or crawl credits.</li>
<li><strong>Skipping the free tools.</strong> Search Console shows data no third-party tool can match, because it comes from Google.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="official-response">Official Response</h2><p>Google does not endorse any paid SEO suite. Its own help page describes Search Console as a free service that helps you monitor and fix your presence in Search. Google&#39;s guidance for site owners puts content quality and technical basics ahead of tools.</p>
<p>Ahrefs and Semrush publish their own price pages. We could not open those pages directly in this research, so the figures above come from review sites and trackers. Where trackers disagree, we show a range or say so.</p>
<h2 id="key-takeaways">Key Takeaways</h2><ul>
<li>Start with free tools: Search Console, Trends, Keyword Planner, free Screaming Frog.</li>
<li>Ahrefs entry plans run from $29 to $129 a month, per TechRadar, with a free plan for verified sites.</li>
<li>Semrush&#39;s classic entry plan is about $140 a month, but trackers vary.</li>
<li>Screaming Frog is free for 500 URLs, and £199 a year for the full licence.</li>
<li>Define the question you need answered before you pay.</li>
<li>Check seats, add-ons and limits.</li>
<li>Prices change, so check each vendor page.</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="is-google-search-console-free">Is Google Search Console free?</h3><p>Yes. Google&#39;s help page describes it as a free service for monitoring and fixing your site&#39;s presence in Search.</p>
<h3 id="what-does-ahrefs-cost-in-2026">What does Ahrefs cost in 2026?</h3><p>TechRadar lists Starter at $29, Lite at $129, Standard at $249 and Enterprise at $1,499 a month. Check Ahrefs&#39;s page for current prices.</p>
<h3 id="what-does-semrush-cost-in-2026">What does Semrush cost in 2026?</h3><p>A DEV Community guide lists $139.95, $249.95 and $499.95 a month for Pro, Guru and Business. Other trackers differ, so check Semrush&#39;s page.</p>
<h3 id="is-screaming-frog-free">Is Screaming Frog free?</h3><p>The free version crawls up to 500 URLs. The paid licence costs £199 a year, per the vendor&#39;s pricing page.</p>
<h3 id="is-keyword-planner-really-free">Is Keyword Planner really free?</h3><p>It is free but needs a Google Ads account, per TechRadar&#39;s review. It shows volume ranges and is built for advertisers.</p>
<h3 id="when-should-i-upgrade-to-a-paid-tool">When should I upgrade to a paid tool?</h3><p>Upgrade when you have a clear question free tools cannot answer, such as competitor keywords or backlinks. Review the value after three months.</p>
