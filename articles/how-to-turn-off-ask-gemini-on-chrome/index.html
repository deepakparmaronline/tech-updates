How to Turn Off Ask Gemini in Google Chrome

Hide the Ask Gemini button in Chrome, manage Gemini permissions, and learn what the setting does and does not disable on personal or managed devices.

Tech UpdatesNov 22, 20268 min read read
Key takeaways
  • You can hide the Gemini button through Chrome Settings under AI innovations and Gemini in Chrome, or unpin it from the toolbar.
  • Hiding the button is not the same as disabling every Gemini feature or deleting saved Gemini activity.
  • Chrome administrators can control Gemini in Chrome on managed devices through Google enterprise policies.
  • Review tab sharing, microphone, location and browsing permissions separately if your concern is privacy rather than the visible button.

If you want to remove Ask Gemini from the Chrome toolbar, open Chrome Settings, go to AI innovations, select Gemini in Chrome and turn off the option to show Gemini at the top of the browser. You can also right click the Gemini button and choose Unpin Gemini. These steps hide the visible entry point. They do not automatically disable every Gemini feature or delete activity stored in your Google Account.

Short Answer

On a supported desktop version of Chrome, open the three dot menu and choose Settings. Open AI innovations, then Gemini in Chrome. Under Preferences, turn off Show Gemini at the top of the browser. If you only want to remove the icon quickly, right click it and choose Unpin Gemini.

Google's Use Gemini in Chrome help page documents the current setting and the available permissions. Chrome Enterprise's release notes also explain that administrators can manage Gemini in Chrome through enterprise policies. The exact controls can vary by device, account and rollout.

What the Question Really Means

People use the phrase turn off Ask Gemini to describe several different goals. Some only want to remove the button from the toolbar. Others do not want Chrome to share the current tab with Gemini. Some want to block the feature for employees or students on managed devices. Others want to delete saved activity or stop a Google Account from using connected information.

These are separate controls. Hiding the icon makes the browser less cluttered, but it does not necessarily switch off the service. Turning off a permission can limit what Gemini can access, but it may leave the button visible. Removing saved activity is an account data action, not a toolbar setting. Choose the control that matches the problem you want to solve.

Google says Gemini in Chrome can use content from the current browser tab to answer questions. On desktop, users can also share additional open tabs. That can be useful for summarising a page, comparing sources or asking questions about a document. It also means that tab sharing and permissions deserve attention when privacy is the reason for turning the feature off.

How It Works

Chrome provides a preference to show or hide Gemini at the top of the browser. Google also documents a right click option to pin or unpin the Gemini control. These are presentation controls. They change how the feature is opened, not necessarily the underlying availability of every AI capability.

The Gemini in Chrome settings page also includes permissions. Google lists precise location, microphone, Share current tab by default and Let Gemini browse for you. These permissions control specific kinds of access or action. They should be reviewed independently. Turning off the toolbar icon does not tell you whether tab sharing, microphone access or browsing permissions remain enabled.

For organisations, Chrome has administrator policies. Google's enterprise release information says administrators can turn off Gemini in Chrome with the GeminiSettings policy or use GenAiDefaultSettings. Policy values and supported platforms can change, so IT teams should use the current policy documentation and test the setting on a pilot device before broad deployment.

Practical Steps

Hide the Ask Gemini button on desktop

  1. Open Google Chrome.
  2. Select the three dot menu in the top right.
  3. Choose Settings.
  4. Open AI innovations.
  5. Select Gemini in Chrome.
  6. Under Preferences, find Show Gemini at the top of the browser.
  7. Turn the preference off.
  8. Close Settings and check whether the button has disappeared.

The exact menu labels can change as Chrome updates. If you cannot find AI innovations, use the Settings search box and search for Gemini. If the setting is not available, check whether your Chrome version and account have received the feature.

Unpin the button from the toolbar

If the Gemini button is visible, right click it and select Unpin Gemini. This is a quick way to remove the icon without searching through the full settings menu. To show it again later, right click the relevant area or return to Gemini in Chrome settings and turn the preference back on.

Unpinning is not a full privacy control. It does not necessarily block the keyboard shortcut, remove the feature from the browser or stop all related AI functionality. Use the permission controls if your concern is what information Gemini can access.

Review tab sharing and permissions

  1. Open Chrome Settings.
  2. Go to AI innovations.
  3. Select Gemini in Chrome.
  4. Review Share current tab by default.
  5. Review Precise location and Microphone.
  6. Review Let Gemini browse for you.
  7. Turn off permissions that you do not want enabled.

A permission can affect the experience. For example, turning off automatic tab sharing may require you to choose a tab when you want Gemini to use its contents. Disabling microphone access can prevent voice features from working. Do not switch off a permission without understanding which feature depends on it.

Disable the feature on a managed device

If you manage Chrome for a business or school, use the current Google Chrome Enterprise policy documentation. Google lists GeminiSettings and GenAiDefaultSettings as controls for Gemini in Chrome. The administrator should confirm the policy scope, supported operating system and policy value before deploying it.

Test the policy on a small group first. Confirm whether the Gemini entry point disappears, whether users can still open related features and whether the setting survives a browser restart. Keep a record of the policy version and the Chrome release used in the test.

Common Problems

Gemini does not appear in AI innovations

Google says Gemini in Chrome is rolling out gradually and may not be available to every user or region. It also has device and sign in requirements. Update Chrome, confirm that you are signed in and check whether your device is supported. A work or school administrator may also block the feature.

The button returns after an update

A browser update, profile change or policy refresh can change the visible controls. Open the Gemini in Chrome settings again and check the preference. If the device is managed, the administrator policy may override a personal setting.

Unpinning does not stop the keyboard shortcut

The toolbar button and keyboard shortcuts are different entry points. Google documents shortcuts for opening and closing Gemini in Chrome on supported desktop platforms. If you want to stop the feature rather than hide the icon, use the available settings or ask the administrator to apply the appropriate policy.

The setting is missing on mobile

The desktop help page describes the toolbar control and the Gemini in Chrome settings on supported desktop platforms. Mobile Chrome may have different controls and a different rollout. Do not follow desktop instructions on a phone if the menu does not match. Review the mobile app's current settings and Google help for the specific device.

What Does Not Work

Unpinning the button does not delete your Gemini history. Clearing Chrome browsing history is not the same as managing Google Account activity. Removing the Gemini app from a phone does not necessarily remove Gemini in Chrome from a desktop browser. Signing out of one Google service may also leave another browser profile signed in.

Do not install unofficial extensions that claim to remove or disable Gemini. They may request broad access to every website you visit. A browser extension is not a substitute for a supported Chrome setting or enterprise policy. Avoid changing experimental Chrome flags unless Google support specifically directs you to a relevant flag for a known issue.

Safety and Limitations

If you use a shared or work device, follow your organisation's policy before changing managed settings. Administrators may need Gemini for approved workflows or may have disabled it for data protection reasons. A local preference can be reset by a managed policy.

If your concern is privacy, review the full data path. Check which tab is shared, whether extra tabs are selected, what permissions are enabled and which Google Account is active. Hiding the button only changes the interface. It does not answer every question about data processing, retention or connected apps.

Key Takeaways

  • Use Chrome Settings, AI innovations and Gemini in Chrome to hide the toolbar control.
  • Right click and choose Unpin Gemini for a quick visual change.
  • Review tab sharing, microphone, location and browsing permissions separately.
  • Managed Chrome environments can use enterprise policies.
  • Hiding Ask Gemini does not delete chats or disable every AI feature.

FAQ

How do I turn off Ask Gemini in Chrome?

Open Chrome Settings, go to AI innovations, select Gemini in Chrome and turn off Show Gemini at the top of the browser. You can also right click the button and choose Unpin Gemini.

Does unpinning Gemini disable the feature?

No. It removes a visible entry point. It does not necessarily disable every Gemini feature, shortcut or permission.

Can I disable Gemini in Chrome completely?

Administrators can use Chrome enterprise policies on managed devices. Personal users should review the controls available in their current Chrome version and account.

How do I stop Gemini from seeing my open tab?

Review Share current tab by default in Gemini in Chrome settings. Turn off the permission if you do not want automatic tab sharing.

Why is Ask Gemini missing from Chrome settings?

The feature is rolling out gradually and may not be available for every device, account or region. A work administrator may also control access.

Does turning off Ask Gemini delete my Gemini chats?

No. Hiding the browser control does not delete saved chats or Google Account activity. Manage activity separately.
