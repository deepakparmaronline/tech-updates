Why Is Claude AI 18? Age Rules and Verification Explained

Claude AI is 18+ because Anthropic sets an adult-only rule for its consumer service. Learn the reason, age checks, and what it means for users in practice.

  • Anthropic says younger users may face a higher risk of adverse effects from chatbot conversations, especially in sensitive situations.

Claude AI is 18+ because Anthropic sets an adult-only rule for its consumer service. The company says younger users face a higher risk of adverse effects from conversations with AI chatbots, especially when a conversation touches on emotional wellbeing or other sensitive topics. This is Anthropic's product policy, not proof that every country has the same legal age rule for every AI service.

Short Answer

Anthropic requires people to be 18 or older to use Claude as a consumer product. Users must confirm their age when creating an account. If Anthropic detects signals that an account may belong to someone under 18, it can disable access and ask the person to verify their age before the account is reinstated.

Anthropic explains its reason in its user wellbeing article. The company says younger users may be at greater risk of adverse effects from chatbot conversations. Its age assurance help page explains how confirmation and further checks work. The rule applies to the consumer Claude service, so it should not be confused with every way an organisation can use Anthropic technology.

What the Question Really Means

When someone asks why Claude AI is 18, they may mean one of three things. They may want to know the minimum age for a Claude account, why Anthropic chose that limit, or why the app asks them to verify their age. Those questions are connected, but the answers are not identical.

The minimum age is the rule Anthropic publishes for its consumer service. The reason is the company's stated view about the risks that chatbot conversations can create for younger users. Verification is the process used when a user needs to confirm that they meet the age requirement. A verification request does not automatically mean that a person has done something wrong. It can mean that the service needs more information before restoring access.

It is also important to separate a company rule from a country's law. Different countries have different rules for online services, privacy, parental consent, and age assurance. Anthropic's 18+ requirement is its published rule for Claude.ai. Do not assume that every AI tool must use the same age limit or that a phone app store rating explains the full policy.

How It Works

The consumer account rule

Anthropic says Claude.ai is available only to people aged 18 or older. During account setup, a user must confirm that they meet this requirement. If a person under 18 identifies themselves as a minor in a conversation, Anthropic says its systems can flag the account for review. Accounts confirmed to belong to minors are disabled.

This policy is separate from the model's ability to answer a question. A model may be capable of explaining a school subject or helping with a coding problem, but that does not mean every person can open a consumer account. Access rules apply to the service, not only to the type of question being asked.

Why Anthropic says it uses the rule

Anthropic says younger users may be more vulnerable to negative effects from AI chatbot conversations. The company points to situations where people use a chatbot for emotional support or discuss sensitive subjects. Its wellbeing article describes work on model behaviour, safety classifiers, and ways to direct users to human support when a conversation suggests that help may be needed.

This is the reason Anthropic gives for its policy. It is not a claim that every young person will be harmed by AI or that an adult account is automatically safe. It is a product decision based on the company's risk assessment. Users should still treat Claude as an AI system with limits and should not rely on it as a replacement for a trusted adult, qualified professional, or emergency service.

What happens during age verification

Anthropic says it may ask a person to verify their age if its systems detect signals that an account may belong to someone under 18. The company's help page says the verification link is sent through a notification email. The user can then use Yoti, a third-party age verification provider.

Yoti offers several methods in supported locations. These include facial age estimation using a selfie, verification with an identity document such as a passport or driving licence, and a Digital ID option for people who already use the Yoti app. The available method can depend on the country and the documents Yoti supports.

Anthropic says Yoti deletes the selfie, document images, and personal data after the age check. Anthropic says it receives only a pass or fail result and does not process or store the verification image or ID document. Read the current age assurance instructions before starting the process.

Practical Steps

If you are creating a new account

Use the official Claude website or app. Enter the correct date of birth and confirm that you are at least 18. Do not use another person's date of birth or account. If you are not old enough to use the consumer service, wait until you meet the requirement rather than trying to get around the check.

If Claude asks you to verify your age

First, check that the message is genuine. Open the official Anthropic notification and follow the age verification link provided there. Do not upload a passport, driving licence, or selfie to a link sent by an unknown account or posted in a public forum. If you are unsure, open the official Claude Help Center yourself and contact support.

Choose one of the methods shown by Yoti in your country. Make sure the image is clear if you use an ID document, and follow the instructions on the verification page. Do not send identity documents to the general chat box or attach them to a message unless official support specifically tells you to use a secure process.

If you believe the flag is wrong

Use the official verification flow and keep the notification email. If the process fails, contact Claude Support and explain that you believe the age check is incorrect. Include the account email and the error message, but do not share more personal information than the support team needs. Follow the current instructions from Anthropic rather than advice from unofficial social media posts.

If you are a developer

Anthropic says organisations that build products for minors using its API must follow additional guidelines. That is a separate situation from a minor signing up for Claude.ai. Developers should read the current child safety and usage guidance, design appropriate safeguards, and obtain legal advice where required. Do not treat API access as a way to bypass the consumer age rule.

Common Problems

A user may be asked to verify even if they are an adult. An automated safety system can make a mistake or may need more information. In that situation, use the official verification process. Creating another account with different details can make the problem harder to resolve and may conflict with the service terms.

A second issue is a mismatch between the document and the account information. Check that the document is supported in your country and that the information is readable. If the verification provider cannot confirm the age, use another method offered on the official page or ask support what to do next.

Some people also confuse the app store age rating with Anthropic's own rule. App stores can display their own ratings and regional requirements. The consumer service may still have a separate minimum age. Always read the current Claude terms and help page rather than relying on a store label alone.

What Does Not Work

Do not use a VPN, a false date of birth, another person's identity document, or a shared account to get around the rule. These steps do not change the service's age requirement and can create account, privacy, and payment problems. Do not pay a third party that promises to unlock an account or complete age verification for you.

Do not assume that a school, parent, or employer account automatically removes the consumer requirement. An organisation may have a separate education or enterprise arrangement, but it must follow the applicable product terms and age guidance. Ask the organisation's administrator and Anthropic Support for the correct route.

Safety and Limitations

Age verification involves personal information, so use only the provider and method named in an official Anthropic notice. Anthropic says Yoti handles the verification data and that Anthropic receives only the result. Read the latest privacy information from both services if you want to understand how the process works in your country.

The 18+ rule does not mean Claude is a substitute for professional help or a safe place to handle every personal crisis. Anthropic describes safeguards that can direct people toward human support, but an AI assistant can still misunderstand context. For urgent risk or mental health concerns, contact a trusted person or a qualified service in your area.

FAQ

Why is Claude AI 18+?

Anthropic says younger users are at a heightened risk of adverse effects from conversations with AI chatbots. It therefore requires consumer Claude users to be 18 or older. This is Anthropic's published product rule.

Is Claude legally required to be 18?

The 18+ requirement is Anthropic's stated rule for its consumer Claude service. Laws and age assurance requirements can differ by country, so do not treat the policy as one universal legal age rule for all AI tools.

Does Claude ask for ID at sign-up?

Users must confirm that they are 18 or older. Anthropic may request further verification if its systems detect signals that an account could belong to someone under 18. The verification method is provided through an official notification.

How does Claude verify age?

Anthropic says it uses Yoti. Available methods include facial age estimation, identity document checks, and a Digital ID option in supported locations. Follow the instructions shown for your country.

Can someone under 18 use Claude through the API?

Anthropic says developers building products for minors with its API must follow additional guidelines. This does not mean a minor can create a consumer Claude account or ignore the 18+ rule.

What should I do if Claude wrongly flags my age?

Use the official age verification link in an Anthropic notification. If the check fails or you think it is a mistake, contact Claude Support through the official Help Center. Never send identity documents to an unofficial website.
