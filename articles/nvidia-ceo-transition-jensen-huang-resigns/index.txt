19:T2dcf,<h2 id="news-summary">News Summary</h2><p>Nvidia&#39;s board announced on <strong>November 12, 2026</strong> that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive officer and a board member, effective immediately. The company appointed its chief financial officer, Colette Kress, as interim CEO while it begins a search for a permanent successor. Harvey C. Jones was appointed Nvidia&#39;s first-ever board chair.</p>
<p>The official <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition">Nvidia newsroom release</a> is dated November 12 but does not display an exact publication time. Reuters&#39; timestamped coverage shows 08:53 a.m. on November 12; the syndicated page does not label a time zone. Nvidia says it expects operations, customer commitments and its product roadmap to continue without interruption.</p>
<h2 id="what-happened">What Happened</h2><p>The board said Huang resigned from three roles: president, CEO and board member. The resignation took effect immediately. The board named Colette Kress, Nvidia&#39;s current executive vice president and CFO, as interim CEO. She will continue to hold the finance role while serving as interim chief executive.</p>
<p>The company also appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever board chair. Jones has been an entrepreneur, technology executive and venture investor, and previously served as CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys. The board said it will consider internal and external candidates for a permanent CEO.</p>
<p>The announcement is a major leadership change for a company that has become central to the AI computing market. Huang co-founded Nvidia more than three decades ago and led it through the growth of graphics processors, accelerated computing and AI infrastructure.</p>
<p>Reuters and the Associated Press reported the resignation on November 12. Their coverage provides additional context around the board&#39;s earlier inquiry and the public reports that preceded the announcement. The official transition release itself does not state why Huang resigned.</p>
<h2 id="why-it-matters">Why It Matters</h2><p>Nvidia is a key supplier of GPUs and accelerated-computing platforms used to train and run AI models. Its products and software are part of the infrastructure used by cloud providers, research labs, enterprises and developers. A change at the top therefore attracts attention well beyond the company&#39;s shareholders.</p>
<p>The immediate question is continuity. Customers plan data-center capacity, software development and long-term purchases around product roadmaps. Nvidia&#39;s release says those commitments and the roadmap are expected to continue without interruption. That is the company&#39;s stated position, not a guarantee that every future project will stay unchanged.</p>
<p>The transition also places the board&#39;s succession process in focus. Kress knows Nvidia&#39;s finance operations and has worked at the company since 2013. The board will now need to decide whether an internal leader or an external candidate should take the permanent role. Until that decision is made, Kress is the interim executive.</p>
<p>The leadership change does not itself announce a new GPU, a new AI model or a change to Nvidia&#39;s developer platform. Those questions should be separated from the CEO news. Investors and customers should look for specific product and earnings updates rather than assume that a management change automatically changes the technology roadmap.</p>
<h2 id="key-details">Key Details</h2><h3 id="colette-kress-takes-the-interim-role">Colette Kress takes the interim role</h3><p>Nvidia says Kress joined the company in September 2013 and has led its finance organization. Before Nvidia, she held finance roles at technology companies including Cisco and Microsoft. She will remain CFO while serving as interim CEO until a permanent successor is named.</p>
<p>That combination gives the company continuity in its financial leadership while the board searches for a permanent chief executive. It does not mean the board has already selected a long-term successor.</p>
<h3 id="harvey-c-jones-becomes-board-chair">Harvey C. Jones becomes board chair</h3><p>The board appointed Jones as its first-ever chair. Nvidia&#39;s release describes him as an entrepreneur, technology executive and venture investor with more than 30 years of experience. He previously served as CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys.</p>
<p>The chair role is separate from the interim CEO role. Kress is responsible for the executive position, while Jones leads the board.</p>
<h3 id="the-next-scheduled-company-update">The next scheduled company update</h3><p>Nvidia&#39;s release says the company plans to report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 financial results on November 17, 2026, at approximately 1:20 p.m. Pacific Time, followed by an earnings call at 2 p.m. Pacific Time. That scheduled event may provide the next formal opportunity for the company to discuss operations and guidance.</p>
<h2 id="who-is-affected">Who Is Affected</h2><p><strong>Cloud providers and data-center operators</strong> will watch for confirmation that product schedules and supply commitments remain on track. Large infrastructure projects depend on hardware availability, software support and long-term planning.</p>
<p><strong>AI developers and research teams</strong> are affected through the wider Nvidia ecosystem, including GPUs, networking and developer software. The CEO announcement does not change a developer&#39;s code or API access by itself, but a leadership transition is relevant to long-term product strategy.</p>
<p><strong>Customers and partners</strong> should continue to use official account, product and support channels for operational information. Nvidia has said existing customer commitments and the product roadmap are expected to continue without interruption.</p>
<p><strong>Investors and analysts</strong> will focus on the succession process, the company&#39;s next earnings update and any future comments from the board or interim CEO. Short-term market reactions should not be confused with a confirmed change in business performance.</p>
<h2 id="what-happens-next">What Happens Next</h2><p>The board will search for a permanent CEO and consider candidates from inside and outside Nvidia. Kress remains interim CEO and CFO until that process is complete. The company has not provided a public deadline for naming a permanent successor.</p>
<p>The next scheduled financial update is November 17. Investors will likely look for comments about demand, supply, customer commitments and the company&#39;s outlook. Those are separate business questions from the reason for the leadership change.</p>
<p>For developers and customers, the practical step is to monitor Nvidia&#39;s newsroom and official product documentation. Do not rely on social posts or unverified claims about product delays. The November 12 release says the company expects its operations and roadmap to continue without interruption.</p>
<h2 id="what-we-know-and-do-not-know">What We Know and Do Not Know</h2><p>We know what the official release says: Huang resigned effective immediately; Kress is interim CEO and CFO; Jones is board chair; and the board is searching for a permanent successor.</p>
<p>We also know that the board issued a statement on November 8 saying it had no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised while it began an independent investigation. On November 10, the independent directors said they had retained outside counsel to conduct that review. Those statements are background; the November 12 transition release did not give a reason for Huang&#39;s resignation.</p>
<p>We do not know the full reason for Huang&#39;s resignation from the official transition release. Reuters and AP report additional allegations and describe the investigation, but those reports are not a substitute for a formal finding. Nvidia&#39;s November 12 release does not say that company secrets were compromised, and the earlier board statement said it had no reason to believe they were.</p>
<p>We also do not know who will become permanent CEO or whether the board will change any part of Nvidia&#39;s strategy after the search. No new chip schedule, customer policy or developer-platform change was announced in the transition release.</p>
